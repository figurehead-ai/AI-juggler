Two screens for the maintainer to approve before they're built (AJ-E3511, AJ-E3512). The Floor window itself is built; these are its controls in the main window and the same facts as an accessible list. Example names are made up.

1 · The Floor button

Beside the bell and your menu, as SPEC §9.7 says. One click opens the Floor (the main part); the small arrow opens a menu with the Floor list. ⌘⇧M opens the Floor from anywhere and, from the Floor, brings this window back. Where the Floor can't run yet (Windows and Linux before their releases), a click says so, and why.

A serious choice, explained: what changes, what doesn't, an example, and a link to the guide. It acts only after the confirm.

Re-create the office?

Every area gets a new look and desk layout. The order stays, so every area stays where you know it.

ChangesEach area's floor, walls, desks, nook and layout, chosen again within its tool's style.
StaysYour Apps, sessions, history, accounts and the main window. Where each area is.
For example: Claude Code · Work keeps its place on floor 1, first column, and stays a workshop; its pinwheel desks may become pods, its planks parquet.

4 · The Floor list

The same facts as the Floor, for a screen reader or the keyboard (accessibility isn't a mode; it's the same information). Grouped by floor, then App, in the Floor's order. Each state is a glyph and a word. Show on the Floor opens the Floor at that desk. New “waiting on you” events are announced politely, once each. Reached from the Floor button's menu and the palette.
