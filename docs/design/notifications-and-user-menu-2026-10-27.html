Notifications and the user menu

For your approval before anything is built. One notification system, specified in the UX spec §4.14 (centre), §2 (Banner, Toast), §4.17 (menu-bar item) and wireframe 27; plus a user menu, which no spec has, designed here to hold Settings, your optional Figurehead account (D-18, D-46), appearance, help and updates. Colours, type and spacing are the app's own.

1 · The header: a bell with one count, and your menu

The bell's number is the same count everywhere — Needs you on Home, the Dock badge and the menu-bar item (§1.1). "Settings" and "More" move into the menu behind your avatar; ⌘, still opens Settings. Navigation keeps Home · Accounts · Usage · History (D-25).

2 · The notification centre (⌘⇧N or the bell)

Everything that happened, past and present, in one place. Needs action mirrors Needs you; Earlier keeps resets, finishes, crashes and updates. Each row says what happened in plain words, offers its one action, and can be snoozed or muted by project or by kind. Nothing here acts by itself — every action is your click.

5 · Your menu (the avatar)

New — no spec had it. It holds what isn't daily work: who you are, your optional Figurehead account, Settings, appearance, help and updates. Grouped as the menu rule says (AJ-E2106): getting there first, then the rest; at most three groups, icons on every item. Without a Figurehead account the avatar shows your Mac's user initial.
