Finding your way: projects, pools, tasks and subagents

Four places you couldn't find or couldn't use in 0.2.0, and the bland menu you found them in. Each section says what's wrong, what changes, and shows it. Nothing is built until you approve; the questions at the end are the ones only you can answer.

Mock-ups use example dataBuilt on what Claude Code, Codex and the others already recordEvery new screen gets a design critique before baselines
At a glance

What's wrong today

Things exist but have no door

  • Grouping by project, pool or account hides behind an unlabelled icon.
  • Projects have no header entry; you reach one only through that grouping or ⌘K.
  • A pool's page opens from Settings › Agent access or a Needs you card; Group by Pool is greyed out "once you make a pool", though every project already has one.
  • Tasks and epics live only on a pool's page.

Things you open don't help

  • Subagents: 437 rows in one run, unexplained indents, no filters, search, usage, result or prompt.
  • The View menu: hints jump between two layouts, no icons, system selects, titles that look like options.
  • Our gates check "unchanged" and "accessible", never "good". The View menu wasn't even captured.

5 · Pools

A pool is one live canvas you watch and edit in place

No separate view and setup screens. Members are nodes, interactions are edges, alerts sit on what they concern with their answers right there. Select anything to see and change it in the inspector. Drag a session from the palette to add it, or onto empty space to start a new pool.

6 · Subagents

Answer "what's running, what failed, what did it cost" first

Why someone opens this: something still running or stuck after the session looks idle; why the session is expensive; what got delegated and what failed; what a background subagent produced; which one started a process. Claude Code forgets finished subagents after 30 seconds and shows no cost per subagent, so the lasting record is our reason to exist here.

  • Speed: that 437-subagent session has about 700 MB of subagent records, and today each page re-reads them all. A small saved index, updated as files grow, makes the tab open at once and page.
  • No double counting: per-subagent tokens are a split of the session's total and show as a share of it.
  • "Running" stays truthful: a subagent with no end record after its session ends reads "Unknown", never a guess; a quiet one shows its last activity.
8 · Building it

How it gets built and checked

  • Shared controls first (choice line, checkbox and radio, picker), so every screen after them is consistent from the start.
  • Then in parallel: navigation (header, rail switch, Projects screen and page), Tasks, Subagents (index, reader, tab, detail), Pools (the canvas).
  • Every new or changed screen gets a design critique next to its screenshot on the review page, against the app's best screens, before you see it.
  • Size: the canvas is the largest piece. Everything else is days, not weeks.

What I need from you

  • Header: Home · Projects · Pools · Tasks · Accounts · Usage · History, with the page title folded into the highlighted tab. OK?
  • Pool canvas layout: you arrange the nodes and it remembers, with an automatic first layout? Or always automatic?
  • One release or two: all of this in 0.3, or ship 0.3 now (stop safety, live limits, the handover line, the Fleet fixes, all ready) and put this redesign in 0.4?
