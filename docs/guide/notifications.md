# Notifications

AI Juggler keeps one list of what needs you and what happened, and shows
it in a few places — always with the same count:

- **The bell** at the top right of the window. Its number is your
  **Needs you** count on Home.
- **The Dock icon** carries the same number.
- **The menu-bar item** shows the same number beside AI Juggler's icon.
  Click it for what needs you and what's open now; a line opens it in AI
  Juggler. Open AI Juggler and New session… are there too.
- **A macOS notification**, only for the kinds you choose (below).

Nothing in any of them acts by itself: every action is your click.

## The notification centre

Click the bell, or press ⌘⇧N.

- **Needs action** is your Needs you list: sessions that ask you or may be
  stuck, a usage limit reached, finished work to review, a sign-in, an
  account almost out of room, an agent's message or task. Each line says
  what happened and offers its action (Open, Review, Hand over…, Sign in…).
- **Earlier** keeps what happened: an account with room again, a session
  that finished, a crash AI Juggler restarted, an update. History is kept
  for 30 days; **Show earlier** reads more.
- **⋯** on an item: **Snooze 1 h**, **Snooze until tomorrow**, **Mute this
  project** or **Mute this kind**. Both stop interrupting you — no dot, no
  banner, no notification. A **snoozed** item also leaves the Needs you
  count until its snooze ends (it says when: "Snoozed · back at 9:00
  tomorrow"), then comes back into every count by itself, even with the
  window closed; **Back into Needs you now** wakes it sooner. A snooze from
  the rail (Snooze for an hour, Mute this session) is the same snooze. A
  **muted** item is still counted. Settings › Notifications › **Muted**
  lists what you muted, with Unmute.
- **Mark all read** clears every dot (the count stays: it's what still
  needs you).
- At the foot: today in one line ("Today: 4 finished · 1 restarted · Work
  Max resets 9:09 PM") and **Notification settings**.

In your quiet hours the centre says so: "Muted until 09:00 — Needs you
still tracks 2."

## Banners

When an account is almost out of room, a banner says so above the screen,
with how much is left and when it resets, and its actions: **Hand over…**
(the session's switch to another account), **Queue for the reset** (you'll
be offered to carry on when it resets; nothing restarts by itself),
**Snooze 1 h**, or close it. A closed banner stays in the centre. On Home
it's an item in Needs you instead.

If AI Juggler's own service stops answering, the window keeps showing the
last state it knew, says since when, pauses what would start something,
and offers **Retry**.

## Choose what reaches you outside AI Juggler

Open Settings › Notifications › **Notify me outside AI Juggler** and turn on
the ones you want:

- **A session waits for you:** it asked a question or wants your approval.
- **A session looks stuck:** no output for a while, with its work
  unfinished.
- **A session hits its usage limit:** its account ran out until the limit
  resets.
- **A session finishes:** it ended on its own, not stopped by you.
- **A session leaves something running:** it ended and left processes
  running, such as a dev server.
- **An account is almost out of room:** its 5-hour or weekly window is
  nearly used up. Once until it resets.
- **A session crashes and is restarted:** if you let AI Juggler restart
  crashed sessions.
- **An account has room again:** its window reset after it was almost out.
- **This Mac is getting tight:** memory, disk or heat near its limit, as
  This Mac shows it.

The first time, macOS may ask whether AI Juggler can notify you. If none
appear, allow AI Juggler in System Settings › Notifications.

## What a notification says

The session's name (its conversation's title, or else its folder), what
happened, and the account, for example "Waiting for your reply · Work Max".
An account's says how much is left: "Work Max is almost out of room · 7% of
its 5-hour window left". Click it to bring AI Juggler forward on that
session, on This Mac, or on the notification centre.

## When you're told

- **Once for each state:** a session that drops out of a state and comes
  back to it within 5 minutes isn't told again (30 minutes for This Mac and
  for an account almost out of room).
- **Never while AI Juggler is in front:** it's already showing you.
- **Not in your quiet hours:** **Quiet hours** hold notifications as well as
  the hourly digest. When they end, you're told about anything still in
  that state.
- **Only for the accounts you choose:** an account left out under **In the
  digest and notifications** stays quiet.
- **Not what you snoozed or muted** in the notification centre.

## Your menu

Your avatar, at the top right, holds what isn't daily work: **Settings**
(⌘,), This Mac, Processes, your other Macs, **Privacy**, **Appearance**
(Light, Dark or Auto), **Keyboard shortcuts**, **Help and guides**,
**Report a problem…** and **About and updates**. Without a Figurehead
account it shows your name from this Mac and one quiet **Connect a
Figurehead account (optional)**; with one, your Cloud backup and your Macs.
