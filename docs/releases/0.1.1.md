AI Juggler 0.1.1 is the first 0.1 release you can update to: it shows every session on your Mac, wherever you started it, and carries a conversation to another account or tool when one runs low.

## New

- **Every session, wherever you started it.** Sessions you started in a terminal or the Claude app show under their projects, live or ended, and Continue in AI Juggler picks one up.
- **Switch a conversation.** When an account runs low, move the conversation to another account or to Codex. The switch screen says first what carries over and what doesn't, and offers the MCP servers and skills the other account lacks.
- **This Mac.** A page of what your AI tools use on this Mac: memory, CPU, swap and each app's windows, with one alert when memory runs short.
- **Monthly caps.** Set a spending cap for an account or a project; AI Juggler warns at 80 % and 100 % in Needs you, and never stops a session.
- **Search the words of every session.** Off by default. Settings › Privacy says how big its index would be before you turn it on, and turning it off deletes it.
- **Keep work going.** Sessions that crash can restart, the Mac stays awake while sessions work, and AI Juggler can open at login and come back after a crash.
- **Coming from another tool.** Projects and settings from Claude Squad and Vibe Kanban come in when you add your accounts.
- **Rules and MCP servers across tools.** Compare a project's rules and servers between tools and accounts, with a diff before anything changes, and undo.
- **Manage accounts, sessions and projects.** Rename, remove and set a default account; rename and pin sessions; forget or move projects.
- **Fleet (beta).** Start a session on another Mac you paired, and see usage per Mac.

## Improved

- **One kind of page.** Accounts, projects, sessions and conversations open the same way, with keys for everything (press ? for the list).
- **Starts faster.** AI Juggler loads only what the first window shows, and the rest as you go.
- **Big transcripts.** A transcript of any size is read a line at a time, so a Mac with very large sessions keeps its memory.
- **Plain words.** Every screen says what it does in plain words, and the Privacy page says what AI Juggler reads, changes and sends.

## Fixed

- **Updating to 0.1.** Updating from 0.0.x to 0.1.0 stopped with "The download didn't pass the signature check". 0.1.0's package was signed without its version, which AI Juggler requires; 0.1.1 is signed with it, and every release is now checked for it before it's published.
- **Codex: subagents and reviews.** Codex's subagents and automatic reviews no longer show as conversations of their own; their usage still counts.
- **This Mac's memory.** Memory used now matches Activity Monitor.
- **First run.** When looking for your setups fails, first run says so and offers Try again, instead of saying none were found.

## Known issues

- **Claude limits.** A Claude account shows how much is left only once a session started in AI Juggler has reported it. Until then it says "Not measured yet", with the tokens used.
- **Some prices are missing.** Codex's automatic reviews and some older models say "No price yet".
- **Fleet** has been checked with two copies of AI Juggler on one Mac, not yet on two Macs.
- **Going back to 0.0.17 or earlier from 0.1.** 0.1 updates AI Juggler's own data. The copy it keeps on the way makes going back possible: Settings › About & updates › Go back to an earlier version. Your Claude and Codex folders aren't affected either way.
