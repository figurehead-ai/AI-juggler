AI Juggler 0.4.9 fixes Move to account, which could be refused on a large conversation and leave its dialog stuck, and what walking 0.4.8 found.

## Fixed

### Switching
- **Move to account waits for both apps to finish closing before it touches the conversation, and its dialog always closes.** A 406 MB conversation's move was refused with "the backup of … doesn't match it", and the dialog then couldn't be closed: the Claude app's Code tab wrote the conversation's last lines after the app's window had closed. A move now waits for every part of both profiles to end (their helpers and the Claude Code they run), then for the conversation's files to hold still, before it backs them up, and tries the backup once more if a last write still landed. When it can't move, it says who was still writing, calmly and in plain words ("Claude app · Work was still writing this conversation as it closed, so nothing was moved. Try again in a moment."), with Retry; the file paths are under Copy details. Esc, × and Cancel close the dialog after a refusal, as Done does after a move. While it runs, one line says what it's doing (waiting for an app to finish closing, backing up, moving, starting the apps again), with a bar while it backs up. A refused or undone move no longer keeps a backup of the whole conversation; those left behind before are removed when AI Juggler starts.

### Sessions and limits
- **After an Account's window resets, nothing still says a session is at its limit.** The header, the sidebar, Home and the bell say "Room again" and offer Resume; a parked session says "Parked" everywhere; Home's heading agrees with its own list. The Parked card's words read as a sentence beside "Resume the same conversation".
- **Every "N min ago" keeps time,** in the sidebar, Home, the bell and a session's notices, and a session's header says when it started in the same words ("started 21 min ago").
- **Home's summary follows a session's state** as it changes, and the Floor shows a session whose limit reset as ready to go on.
- **A session in a setup you just brought in reads as running at once,** never as ended for half a minute.
- **A session that had no turn yet when you updated from 0.4.5 or older starts again** instead of failing once.

### New session
- **"Run without a terminal" is remembered** for the App you chose it on (Beta, where the App can use it).
- **The sheet holds still when its warning goes:** after you pick another App, the warning's place says what changed, with Undo.

### Pools
- **A project picked under one template is kept only where the new template uses it the same way,** and the step says when it was cleared.
- **A session reads by one name everywhere** (the diagram, the List view, the seal and Who's in).
- **A member that works through its shell says so plainly:** it can't read the pool's messages, and its tool doesn't report what it's doing. The setup's lead row says "Its next session leads; the role here is for its other sessions."
- **With Add a member open, the planned team fits beside it.**

### This Mac
- **A nearly full swap beside "Plenty of room" says why it doesn't count yet,** and the Overview shows "Measuring this Mac" until its first readings, never "No AI tools are running" before it has looked.
