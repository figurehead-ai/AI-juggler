AI Juggler 0.5.0 adds Bot, which answers questions about your work, plus watches that tell you when something you care about happens, an AI models area that stays on your Mac, and a calmer look everywhere.

## New

- **Ask Bot about your work.** The Bot icon at the top right (⌘J) opens one conversation that answers questions about your sessions, tasks, pools and usage: "What's blocked?", "What finished today?", "How much of Work did I use?". It looks things up in AI Juggler's own records and links to each one, says what it looked at, and says plainly when it doesn't know. It never talks to your agents or changes anything. Expand opens it as a full page, with search, New topic and Clear.
- **Ask about one thing.** "Ask Bot about this" on a session, an App or a project opens Bot about that one ("About: api"); one click on the × widens it to everything again.
- **Bot can write a short answer, on your Mac, if you want it.** With Apple Intelligence on, or a tested model downloaded, turn on Written answers (Beta): Bot writes a sentence or two above the records it found, and shows an answer only when every claim in it matches those records. Your questions never leave your Mac.
- **Bot remembers what you tell it to.** "Remember: call web the api agent" or "Remember: shop matters most" keeps a note that Bot uses from then on; What Bot remembers lists each one, and Forget removes it for good.
- **Watches tell you when something happens.** Watch a session, an App, a project or an account, and AI Juggler tells you when it finishes, asks you something, fails, gets stuck or runs low, once or until you stop it. The Watches page lists them all, to edit, pause or remove, with Undo. A watch can ring even during your quiet hours, or wait until they end. Bot sets one up too ("Watch this…"), after you confirm what it will watch.
- **Fewer notifications at once.** When several sessions finish together you get one notification that names them all, and what waited during your quiet hours arrives as one.
- **Home shows what happened.** Home is now an activity feed: what needs you stays pinned at the top, then what your agents did, newest first. Your Apps and each account's room are on the right. A digest sums up what happened while you were away.
- **An AI models area that stays on your Mac.** Settings › AI › Models recommends the best model for your Mac: Apple's own model when Apple Intelligence is on, with nothing to download. Otherwise it shows a short list of models AI Juggler has tested, each with its size and what it's good at. A download starts only when you click it, checks the file it gets, and asks you again before a second model. Each one has a Remove, and every AI job has an off switch.
- **Each App keeps its own account.** A Claude app profile and Claude Code CLI on one folder, signed in to two different accounts, now each show their own account and its room. Before, both showed the account that ran last. Limits are counted for the account they were read under.

## Improved

- **A calm look.** Colour now means something needs you: the rail, the header and Home show amber or red only for what needs you or has run out, and stay quiet otherwise. Back and Forward are always in the top bar, and each list says when more is waiting below.
- **The welcome names the tools AI Juggler has proven with real accounts:** Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode and Grok Build. The rest still come in on first run, and are named once each has been checked the same way.

## Fixed

- **Settings names a Claude app profile as that profile.** Automatic cleanup, renewal dates, notifications and Usage named a folder only a Claude app profile uses "Claude Code CLI · …". They now name the profile.

## Known issues

- **Going back to 0.4.6.** 0.5.0 updates AI Juggler's own data. The copy it keeps on the way makes going back possible: Settings › About & updates › Go back to an earlier version. That copy is from before the update, so what you set up in 0.5.0, such as your watches and what Bot remembers, isn't in it. A model you downloaded stays on your Mac after going back, and 0.4.6 doesn't list it: remove it first in Settings › AI › Models to get the space back.
- Turn reviews aren't available yet: Settings › AI › Models shows them as Not yet.
- Local models need a Mac with Apple silicon.
- The Floor is on macOS only. Windows gets it in 0.6 and Linux in 0.7.
