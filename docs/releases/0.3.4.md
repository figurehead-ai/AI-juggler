AI Juggler 0.3.4 shows a session's conversation the way the tools' own apps do, puts its subagents one click away, and makes switching pages steady.

## New

- **Switch to a subagent's conversation from the session's own.** On a session's Overview, a column on the left lists what the tools' own apps list with it: **This session** first, then the subagents **Running** now, the sessions it **Started from here** (from the Claude app's own record, as its sidebar nests them), and **Done**, folded, with the latest that ended. One click shows that conversation in place; **Esc** or **Back** returns. The session's terminal stays exactly as it was meanwhile. The column shows only when there's something besides the conversation, and the **Subagents** tab stays the full list.

## Improved

- **Switching pages is steady.** A page you come back to shows what it had at once and reads again behind it, keeps where you'd scrolled, and keeps its sections' space while they load. The first open of Projects, Usage, History or Tasks no longer flashes blank. Accounts no longer shows a skeleton on every visit. Data still refreshes on its own schedule and after anything you change.

- **A conversation reads like the app's own window.** Each finished turn shows your message, one row for its work, then the reply. The row says how long it took and what it did, such as "Worked for 4 min · Ran 3 commands, edited a file · 2 notes". The notes written between steps and the steps themselves are one click away, as written. While a session works, its latest turn stays open as it goes. The Claude app and ChatGPT's Codex show a turn the same way.
- **Dark and easy to read.** In the dark theme a conversation sits on a near-black, neutral surface, not white text on blue-grey. In the light theme it's plain white. This applies to a session's conversation and to a subagent's.
- **The question is the reply's.** When a session asks you something, the card at the bottom quotes the question in its reply, never one from the notes it wrote while working.

## Fixed

- **The Subagents tab no longer opens on an empty list.** It opens on **Running** when something runs and on **All** otherwise, decided each time you open the tab. Before, it decided when the page opened and could still show "Nothing is running now" long after everything had finished.
- **Its counts add up.** The summary now says how many failed, how many were stopped and how many have no recorded end. Together these make up **Needs attention**. Before, "18 failed" sat beside "Needs attention 19" with nothing saying what the 19th was.
- **Subagents with no type can be found.** **Type** offers **No type recorded** when some have none.
- **Another app's session page names its subagents.** A session the Claude app or ChatGPT runs now shows "259 subagents · 1 running › Subagents" under its title, as AI Juggler's own sessions do.

## Known issues

- Clicking an app's name in the rail's **App** grouping opens its folder's page, titled Account. The app's own page, with only its sessions, comes in 0.4.
- A session it started shows in the list only while its app is open (the app's own record is read from its running window, as its sidebar is).
