AI Juggler 0.3.3 opens apps that aren't running, and can keep the ones you choose running.

## New

- **Open starts an app that isn't running.** AI Juggler remembers each desktop app and profile it has seen running: the Claude app's profiles, ChatGPT (Codex). Settings › Desktop apps › **Your apps and profiles** lists them. **Start** opens one that isn't running, on the same profile; **Open** brings a running one forward. Before, Open only worked while the app was already running.
- **Open in the app an ended session was in.** An ended session's page offers **Open in Claude · Work** (or wherever it was last open). AI Juggler starts that app if it's closed. The Codex app opens at the thread itself; in the Claude app, you pick the session in its sidebar.
- **Keep alive, per app or profile.** Turn it on in Settings › Desktop apps or from a right-click on the app's place in the rail. When that app or profile stops running (a crash, ⌘Q, Force Quit), AI Juggler starts it again about 10 seconds later, on the same profile, behind the app you're in. Each start is a notice with the way to turn it off. Home shows a tile for a kept-alive app that's down, with Start, and "kept alive" on one that's running.
- **Keep alive knows when not to.** Never while your screen is locked, in the first minute after AI Juggler starts or your Mac wakes, or while AI Juggler is quitting, so logging out and shutting down work as always. After 3 starts in 10 minutes it pauses and says why; opening the app again ends the pause. Stopping the app from AI Juggler pauses it too.

## Safe by design

- An app is started exactly as it last ran: the app, and the profile folder it was given. Never another account, never anything typed into it.
- Before every start, AI Juggler checks the app is still where it was and that no other user can change it.
- The list of apps is AI Juggler's own record; the window can't rewrite it. A profile's sign-in is the app's; AI Juggler doesn't read it, so a signed-out profile starts at its own sign-in.
