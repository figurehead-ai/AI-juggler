AI Juggler 0.5.1 fixes what testing 0.5.0 found.

## Fixed

- **No "(null) differs from previously opened versions" prompt when Bot starts.** AI Juggler's helpers now carry its name, and a Dev build's helpers never share an identity with the ones the released app signs.

## Clearer words and fewer doubles

- **One name per thing.** A watch on a turn's end now reads "Its turn ends", apart from a session that finishes; the bell and Processes say **Needs you**; the top bar says "This Mac: fine" so "room" stays the accounts' word; Bot's switch is **Written answers** in Bot and in Settings.
- **"While you were away" and the digest are two things.** The card at the top of Home is **While you were away**; **the digest** is the few lines every 15 to 30 minutes while your sessions work. The digest now counts sessions, not "agents", and tells two sessions with one name apart.
- **The bell is shorter.** It holds Needs you and the latest few events, with **See all on Home**; the 30-day history is in Home's feed.
- **Remind me makes a watch.** On a low-room banner, Remind me now makes a one-time "Room again" watch you can see, edit and remove on Watches.
- **Settings › Privacy** has **Check for configuration updates** (on; off, nothing is fetched) with what it fetches, and lines for Bot's notes and for watches. The providers' terms are behind a closed **Providers’ terms** everywhere.
- **Models** lists only the jobs a model can do today, and its job switches live there (the separate Jobs page is gone).

## Known issues

- Nightly testers who ran a Dev build may see a one-time macOS prompt about a changed helper; it is AI Juggler's own signed helper.
