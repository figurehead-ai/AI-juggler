AI Juggler 0.4 adds the Floor, a live office in its own window that shows all your AI work at a glance and takes you to what needs you in one click.

## New

- **The Floor.** Open it with the Floor button in the header, Window › The Floor, the menu bar icon or ⌘⇧M. Each app is an area of an office, each session a person at a desk, each subagent a helper beside them, with your accounts and your Mac in the lobby. It opens on the floor with the app that most needs you.
- **Everything on the Floor means something.** Someone waving with a `!` is waiting on you; a `?` is probably stuck; smoke is a crash; someone asleep has hit a limit. A small fire marks an app using too much of the processor, a battery on an area's sign shows its account running low, and the lobby's rack shows how hard your Mac is working. Every state also has a symbol and a word, never colour alone. Click a person, a sign, the rack or the notice board and AI Juggler opens it.
- **Areas you can find again.** Each app's area has its own look, chosen once and kept, with the tool's logo and the account's initials printed on its floor and the app's name on its sign. Areas stay where they are: they grow with their sessions and only move forward when an app before them changes size. Each pool has a meeting room.
- **Move around from the trackpad or the keyboard.** Drag to pan, pinch to zoom, twist or use Q and E to turn. Tab moves between areas, N jumps to the next session waiting on you, Enter opens it, and 1 to 9 pick a floor.
- **Settings › The Floor:** open it when AI Juggler starts, keep it on top, how many apps fit on a floor, how much it moves, and Re-create the office… for a new look.
- **The Floor list** in the main window shows the same in words, by floor and app, and works with VoiceOver and the keyboard.

## Improved

- **The Floor costs next to nothing.** It draws only when something changes or moves, stops drawing when it's covered or minimised, and frees its memory when you close it. Nothing runs for it until you first open it.

## Known issues

- The Floor is on macOS only. Windows gets it in 0.6 and Linux in 0.7.
- **Going back to 0.3 from 0.4.** 0.4 updates AI Juggler's own data. The copy it keeps on the way makes going back possible: Settings › About & updates › Go back to an earlier version. That copy is from before the update, so what changed in 0.4, such as the Floor's layout, isn't in it. Your Claude and Codex folders aren't affected either way.
