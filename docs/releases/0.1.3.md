AI Juggler 0.1.3 shows what is open on your Mac first, in the app and profile each session runs in, with one click to get there.

## New

- **What's open, where.** The rail and Home now list the sessions that are really running, grouped by the app and profile they run in: each Claude app profile, the Codex app, your terminals, and AI Juggler itself. Sessions that ended, sessions an app lists but isn't running, and background runs fold into one line at the bottom, "Also: …", which shows them with a click.
- **Go to it.** Click or press Return on a session and AI Juggler brings its app and profile to the front, and says what it did. Codex threads open in the Codex app.
- **Needs you first.** A session asking you something, a limit reached, a sign-in, a busy Mac: everything waiting on you is one list at the top, each with its own button.
- **Last messages.** A session's page shows its last few messages, read from its transcript when you open it, with secrets taken out. Nothing is kept. You can turn this off in Settings › Desktop apps.
- **View.** One button (or ⇧V) groups the rail by place, project or account, and shows ended, inactive or background sessions when you want them.
- **Accounts and profiles.** An account is a subscription; a profile is a setup an app runs on. The Accounts page shows each account once, with its profiles beneath.

## Improved

- **Menus.** Every menu has icons, puts getting there first and stopping last, and says why something can't be done right now. Right-click opens the same menu.
- **Settings.** Settings is a list of panes with sections, and every setting kept its place in one of them.
- **Keys.** ⌘1–⌘9 go to the places in the rail; Home is ⌘0, Accounts ⌘⇧A, Usage ⌘⇧U and Fleet ⌘⇧F. The key that opens AI Juggler from anywhere has no default; choose one in Settings › Desktop apps.

## Fixed

- **Reading less.** AI Juggler no longer reads the environment of other programs to tell which profile a session uses. It reads the apps' own session files instead.
- **The right profile.** Bringing the Claude app forward picks the right profile when several are open.
- **Pinned sessions.** A pinned session that had ended no longer disappears from the rail.
- **200 % zoom.** A session's page scrolls as a whole when the window is small, so nothing is cut off.
