AI Juggler 0.4.3 makes every running app open to its sessions and mark its account when it is out of room, even with nothing open in it.

## Fixed

- **A running app with nothing open shows its sessions.** Under Open now, an app or profile that runs with no session open used to show only its name, and its arrow opened nothing. It now lists its last three sessions under it, then "Show all" to open the app's page with the rest. An app with sessions open lists those first; its earlier ones are behind "Last 3 inactive".
- **An arrow only where there is something to show.** An app with no sessions at all says "nothing open" and has no arrow. Folding an app now hides everything under it, its pinned sessions too.
- **Folds you didn't mean are undone.** In 0.4.2, clicking the arrow of an app with nothing open was saved as folding it. Those folds are cleared once, so those apps open as you asked.
- **The Out mark follows the app's account.** An app whose account is out of room, or low, shows the mark under Open now, under Inactive apps, on Home's app cards and in Accounts' Apps, whether or not anything is open in it. Before, the mark came from the app's first open session, so an app with nothing open never showed it, and an app could show another account's mark. Screen readers now hear the mark too.
- **An app's sessions are the ones it lists.** A Claude app profile's sessions in AI Juggler are now the sessions its own Code sidebar has. Sessions you deleted in the app are no longer counted under it, so its count matches the app. They are still in History. A profile you don't have open now hides the sessions you archived in it, as one that's open did already.
- **Counts stay right after an update.** AI Juggler keeps a list of your sessions so it starts fast. After 0.4.2 changed how it tells a desktop session from a terminal one, sessions it had listed before kept the old answer, so some terminal apps counted sessions they never had. The list is now read again whenever the way sessions are read changes.
- **A forked conversation is its own session.** A conversation forked from another in the Claude app used to merge with its parent into one row. Each is now its own row, as in the app.
- **Search and filters find past sessions.** Searching, or showing one app only, now also finds the earlier sessions of an app that is running, under that app.
- **A wrong Out mark after an early reset.** When an account's room came back before its stated reset time, AI Juggler kept it marked out until then. It now follows the account's newest reading.

## Known issues

- The first start after updating reads all your sessions again, once. The window opens as usual and the lists fill in as they're read.
- Going back to 0.4.2 works as before (Settings › About & updates).
- The Floor is on macOS only. Windows gets it in 0.6 and Linux in 0.7.
