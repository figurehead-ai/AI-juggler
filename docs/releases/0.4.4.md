AI Juggler 0.4.4 is a round of fixes.

## Fixed

- **One time for when an account has room again.** An account out of room on both its five-hour window and its week said three different times: its five-hour reset on the rail and Home's apps, its weekly reset in Needs you, and the five hours again under Runway by account. It can't work until the later one, so every place now says that time: the rail, Home's apps and Runway, Needs you, New session, the account's page, the limit window and the Floor. When that time truly isn't known, each says so.
- **Measure its limits measures.** On Home, in Accounts, on an account's page and on a session's usage, "Measure its limits" only opened another page. It now asks the tool for the account's limits right there, says "Measuring…", and shows the reading where the button was. If the check fails, it says why. If asking is turned off for that app, it says so, with Turn on. For a tool that can't be asked, it says the limits show once a session on it reports them.
- **A limit hit stays with the account it happened on.** After you moved a conversation to another account, a limit it had hit before the move could count against the new account, which then showed as out of room as soon as you moved there. Each limit hit now counts for the account it happened on.
- **An app you added is kept.** An app or profile you added, or one on your accounts, was forgotten after 90 days without running, with the name you gave it. Now only apps AI Juggler found running and you never added are forgotten.
- **Codex threads under the right app.** A long Codex thread could appear under Codex CLI for a moment before moving under the Codex app, where it started. It now appears in the right place straight away.

## Known issues

- Limit hits counted against the wrong account before 0.4.4 stay in that account's history.
- Going back to 0.4.3 works as before (Settings › About & updates).
- The Floor is on macOS only. Windows gets it in 0.6 and Linux in 0.7.
