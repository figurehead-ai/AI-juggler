AI Juggler 0.4.4 is a round of fixes.

## Fixed

- **One time for when an account has room again.** An account out of room on both its five-hour window and its week said three different times: its five-hour reset on the rail and Home's apps, its weekly reset in Needs you, and the five hours again under Runway by account. It can't work until the later one, so every place now says that time: the rail, Home's apps and Runway, Needs you, New session, the account's page, the limit window and the Floor. When that time truly isn't known, each says so.
- **Measure its limits measures.** On Home, in Accounts, on an account's page and on a session's usage, "Measure its limits" only opened another page. It now asks the tool for the account's limits right there, says "Measuring…", and shows the reading where the button was. If the check fails, it says why. If asking is turned off for that app, it says so, with Turn on. For a tool that can't be asked, it says the limits show once a session on it reports them.
- **A limit hit stays with the account it happened on.** After you moved a conversation to another account, a limit it had hit before the move could count against the new account, which then showed as out of room as soon as you moved there. Each limit hit now counts for the account it happened on.
- **Past limit hits move back too.** Limit hits that earlier versions counted against the account a conversation was moved to are put back on the account they happened on, once, when 0.4.4 first starts. A hit from an account you've since removed counts for no account.
- **An app you added is kept.** An app or profile you added, or one on your accounts, was forgotten after 90 days without running, with the name you gave it. Now only apps AI Juggler found running and you never added are forgotten.
- **Codex threads under the right app.** A long Codex thread could appear under Codex CLI for a moment before moving under the Codex app, where it started. It now appears in the right place straight away.
- **A finished session shows what it kept.** Reopening a session that ended said "scrollback replayed" over an empty terminal. It now shows the terminal only when its output was kept, and otherwise the conversation itself; with neither, it says no output was kept and offers to search History.
- **Ended times carry their day.** "Finished 4:22 PM" on a session that ended days ago now reads "Thu, Nov 5 4:22 PM", or "yesterday 6:24 PM". Home's "In the last 12 hours" writes its times the same way, without a weekday for today.
- **Newest first, by project too.** Grouped by project, a project's ended sessions listed the oldest first. They now list the newest first, as by app and by account.
- **Projects say what they count.** The Projects list's "This week" column was tokens and read "None" beside a project with 14 sessions, and its Sessions column counted only the open ones. The columns now read "Tokens this week" and "14 · 3 open". A project whose sessions recorded no token use says so on its Usage tab, rather than "once a session has run".
- **The rail holds still.** A Needs you notice that arrived late moved the rail's rows by about 90 px under the pointer when the rail was mostly empty. The row under the pointer now stays put, with "1 new above".
- **An app's state reads whole.** An app that runs with nothing open says "nothing open" on its header at the rail's usual width, where the words used to give way to the app's name. An app whose own list has no sessions says so, not "once AI Juggler has seen them".
- **Needs you on Home names the app that owns it.** Home's Needs you list now names a session's app as the rail does ("Claude Code CLI · Work"), not the folder it ran in.
- **Search and filters by project find every app's sessions.** Grouped by project, searching or showing one account only hid other apps' sessions in that project. They now show.
- **A pinned session that ended stays in sight by account.** Grouped by account, a pinned session that had ended showed nowhere. It now shows under its account, or under Pinned when that account has nothing open.

- **A session's header stays readable in a narrow window.** Its name, account and app gave way to the buttons beside them, down to "Keep th…" and "· W". The buttons now go down a line first, and the chips shorten in steps, the app's name last.
- **Esc returns to where you were.** Closing "Continue in another app…" with Esc could leave the keyboard in the session's terminal instead of on what opened it.

## Known issues

- Going back to 0.4.3 works as before (Settings › About & updates).
- The Floor is on macOS only. Windows gets it in 0.6 and Linux in 0.7.
