The first beta of AI Juggler: one free Mac app to run, watch and switch between your Claude Code accounts, built around what needs you first.

## New

- **Home**: what needs you (each with the one action that moves it on), what's running, how much each account has left, and where you left off.
- **First run in three steps**: AI Juggler finds the Claude Code setups already on your Mac (the terminal and the Claude desktop app's profiles), brings them in as copies with one button, helps you sign each one in, then asks for the Mac permissions it can use. Your originals are never changed, and "Where we looked" shows what it checked.
- **One list of your sessions**: grouped by project, with Needs you, Running and All up front. Everything else is behind Filters, and the status guide is behind "?".
- **Runway in words**: Accounts and Usage show each account's 5-hour window and week (what's left and when it resets), when your pace would run out, and the best account for new work.
- **When an account hits its limit**: one decision. Continue now on another account, wait for the reset (the session is parked and offered back to you), or choose another.
- **Switching** (beta): move a conversation and its project context to another account, and switch back later. Before you confirm, the switch shows what carries over and what doesn't.
- **Sessions up close**: each session is named by its branch or folder. Switch account sits beside Stop, and Details holds the rest: usage, history, health and the processes it owns.
- **Cleanup**: every process AI Juggler starts is tracked until it exits. Leftovers are checked before anything is stopped, and every stop is recorded. Automatic cleanup stays off until you turn it on for an account.
- **Worktrees**: run a second session in the same project, in its own git worktree, without the two getting in each other's way.
- **History**: search past sessions, replay them read-only, archive them, and choose how long each account keeps them.
- **Backup and restore**: save AI Juggler's own data to one file and restore it, for example on a new Mac.
- **Fleet** (beta): pair your own Macs with a code, then see and manage their sessions from one window.
- **Updates**: AI Juggler checks for a new version when it starts (you can turn this off), shows what changed, and installs only when you choose. Pick Stable or Beta in Settings › About & updates.
- **AI Juggler Nightly**: a separate app with the newest build every night. It installs next to AI Juggler and keeps its own data.
- **Optional Figurehead account**: connect one for end-to-end encrypted cloud backup and for finding your Macs for Fleet. Everything else works without an account.
- **Privacy**: usage statistics are off unless you turn them on, and Settings › Privacy shows exactly what would be sent.
- **Keyboard and zoom**: every screen works with the keyboard alone and at 200% zoom, in light and dark themes. List density is in Settings › Appearance.

## Known issues

- Only Claude Code is supported in this beta. Codex and ZCode are planned.
- AI Juggler needs macOS 14 or later.
- Switching is beta. Read what the switch says will carry over before you confirm it.
- Fleet is beta and has had little testing across two Macs so far.
- Crash reports are kept on your Mac in this build. Nothing is sent.
- Connecting a Figurehead account hasn't been tried against the live service yet. If it doesn't connect, everything else in AI Juggler works without an account.
- The app icon is a placeholder.
