AI Juggler 0.4.8 fixes what walking 0.4.7 found: a session's page shows what it already printed, limits read honestly after a reset and for tools that don't report them, and the pool setup works for every way of pointing and clicking.

## Fixed

### Sessions
- **A session's terminal shows what it already printed when you open its page**, from Home, the sidebar or anywhere else. Before, a session that asked a question and waited could open on a blank terminal: what it printed between the page reading its saved output and starting to listen was lost. The terminal now joins the two without losing or repeating anything, and says "Reading what it printed" until it has drawn it, rather than showing Live over an empty screen.
- **"Started … ago" keeps time.** A session page's header could say "started 1s ago" for minutes while you moved between apps; its clock now keeps its pace, and a new session reads "started just now".

### Limits and This Mac
- **An Account doesn't read "almost out of room" after its window resets.** Once a window's reset time has passed, its reading from before no longer counts as low, and the notice says which window has room again.
- **A tool that doesn't report its limits gets words, not full bars.** A session's Usage tab showed "32k of 32k · 100%" bars for a tool whose limits AI Juggler doesn't know; it now says "This session: 32k of the 32k new tokens AI Juggler saw on this app" and why there's no limit to show.
- **This Mac lets go of a process you stopped.** Its Overview kept saying "1 process left running after its session closed" after you stopped it on Processes; it now updates within seconds.

### Pools
- **The pool setup's steps and Cancel can be reached by any pointer.** The full-window setup now sits above the rest of the window where it's drawn, so its left-hand steps and Cancel respond to Voice Control, Switch Control and other ways of pointing, not only to a mouse. The rest of the window waits behind it.
- **A template card selects with a press anywhere on it**, and Next waits until a project is picked ("Pick a project to go on.") instead of making a pool with none.
- **A pool's ⋯ (Rename, Delete) is the last control in its header.**
- **The Pools grid keeps its order after you delete a pool.**
