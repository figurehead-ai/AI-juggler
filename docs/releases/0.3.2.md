Now it says what's true: "Codex is using its credits", with how many are left and when the window resets. It's told once, under Earlier in Notifications, and doesn't count as something that needs you; Continue on another account… is still there. The switch list, New session, the limit dialog, Home and the account card say "Using credits · 14,150 left", and that a session there goes on with its credits until the window resets. An account on its credits comes after any with room in its window and before any that's truly out, and it's never the Suggested one. With no credits, or a balance of 0, nothing changes.
