AI Juggler 0.3.2 fixes what was found in 0.3.0 and 0.3.1.

## Fixed

- **An account on its credits isn't "out of room".** A Codex account whose week was used up kept working on its pre-bought credits, while AI Juggler said it had run out of room and offered to move the work. Now it says what's true: "Codex is using its credits", with how many are left and when the window resets. It's told once, under Earlier in Notifications, and doesn't count as something that needs you; Continue on another account… is still there. The switch list, New session, the limit dialog, Home and the account card say "Using credits · 14,150 left", and that a session there goes on with its credits until the window resets. An account on its credits comes after any with room in its window and before any that's truly out, and it's never the Suggested one. With no credits, or a balance of 0, nothing changes.
- **Credits that run out mid-window say so.** An account carrying on with its credits is watched: when the credits run out while its window is still used up, the real "has run out of room" notice comes. A credits balance read more than 6 hours ago only "probably" covers (it may have run out since), and the room line says how old a reading is ("as of 2:10 PM"). Another Mac you've paired hears that the account carries on with its credits.
- **Banners take one line of the page.** The most pressing banner shows; the rest wait behind "2 more notices", opened and folded in place. The page under it is never cut off.
- **Close and Snooze answer at once.** A banner's × took about a minute in 0.3.0. Now it goes in the same moment (measured: 7 ms) and the backend catches up; if the backend refuses, the banner comes back with the reason. Settings › Advanced › Report a problem shows each backend call's slowest and latest time.
- **"Continue on another account…" opens the choice.** From a banner, Home, a row's menu, ⌘⇧H or Switch account…, the ranked accounts open in a dialog over the page, the suggested one ready for Return. It works the same for a session AI Juggler runs and one another app runs. Esc or a click outside closes it.
- **"Remind me at 12:36 AM" replaces "Queue for the reset".** It sets a reminder for each open session on that account, AI Juggler's or another app's. Nothing shows while it waits. When the account has room again, a banner names the session and opens it. In 0.3.0 the reminder vanished as soon as it was due, and its Resume opened New session instead.
- **Switching from another app's session checks first.** Before anything changes, the switch reads the conversation it will carry and checks that the target account's tool is ready: its files readable, its first-run setup done. If macOS keeps a folder from AI Juggler, it says which folder, which setting to allow (Privacy & Security › Files & Folders), and that nothing was changed.
- **The switched conversation is found.** The copy went into a folder Claude Code doesn't look in whenever the project's path held a space or a dot, so the new session started empty, on Claude Code's setup screen. The folder is now named exactly as Claude Code names it.
- **AI Juggler watches that the conversation arrived.** After a switch it checks, for up to 30 seconds, that the new session opened the conversation. If the new session closes first, or opens another conversation, a notice says so; the original is untouched.
- **The switch says where the work goes on.** From a session in the Claude app, the confirm says it continues here in AI Juggler, in a terminal, not in the Claude app, and the button reads "Continue here in AI Juggler". Continuing inside the Claude app itself comes once it's been proven on real profiles.
- **The terminal draws Claude Code properly.** Its frames, bars and block characters were drawn from a fallback font and looked broken. The terminal now draws them itself (WebGL), with fonts the Mac finds.
- **Processes folds every row.** The app row and a session's row fold and unfold on the click. The table keeps its rows while it reads, instead of going blank.
- **The top bar stays one row.** With the session list open, a longer This Mac state ("Under pressure") or Fleet's tab pushed the bar onto two rows. Now This Mac's words shorten first, and on a narrow bar the cleanup pill shows a broom and its count.
- **Smaller things the re-render caught.** The rail shows a session's age whole or not at all, never "· 7 …". Row menus are wide enough for "Continue on another account…". The "Not signed in yet" line on a session's page wraps instead of running off its card. A crash report wraps its long lines. The port warning has its background back. Loading placeholders show on the light theme's grey page. And the overload advice no longer says "Close the The Claude app windows".
- **Processes fits a 1024-pixel window.** The table ran past its card, cutting off each row's Stop… and Clean up…. On a narrow table, Age and the CPU line now give way, and every button stays whole, with the session list open too.
- **A second look caught more.** The terminal's margin is dark like the terminal, so its text no longer touches the edge in the light theme. A rail row keeps its time whole and shortens the project instead ("ai-jug… · 3 h ago"). Home's first need gives its reason on two lines instead of cutting it off. Commands wrap between words, not mid-word. The shortcut sheet says "More below" when its list runs past the window. The light theme's loading placeholders are a step darker.
- **No "Reload" menu.** Right-clicking where AI Juggler has no menu of its own showed the web view's menu. Now nothing shows there. Text fields, the terminal and selected text keep Copy and Paste. The rail's group headers have their own menu: Open its page, Bring it forward, Fold.

## Changed

- **Colours you can read at a glance.** Cards stand off the page in both themes (a grey page under white cards in light). Text has four levels: headings and values brightest, sentences a step down, labels dimmer, hints dimmest. Every level clears WCAG AA on every surface (the dark violet is a touch lighter for it). The main button is one solid colour, in dialogs too.
- **Pickers show what you'd pick first.** On a long list, the most used and related come first ("With members", "Recent"), then the whole list, with search from nine items; empty ones go last, and "All" sits on top. The Tasks filters, New pool's project and Link a pool use it first.
