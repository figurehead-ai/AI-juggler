The first beta of AI Juggler: one free Mac app to run, watch and switch between your Claude Code accounts, and to clean up after them.

## New

- **Accounts side by side**: add several Claude Code accounts, each in its own folder, and start an Instance from any project in one click.
- **Smart onboarding**: AI Juggler finds the Claude Code setups already on your Mac, imports read-only copies, and walks you through each account's own sign-in. It never signs in for you and never stores your passwords.
- **Switching** (beta): when an account runs out of usage, move the conversation and its project context to another account, and switch back later. Before you confirm, the switch shows what carries over and what doesn't.
- **Usage you can trust**: each account's 5-hour and weekly windows, a suggestion to switch before you run out, and renewal dates if you enter them. Anything that can't be measured says "unknown".
- **Inspector**: select any account, Instance, session or project to see its usage, history, health and the processes it owns.
- **Cleanup**: every process AI Juggler starts is tracked until it exits. Leftovers are checked before anything is stopped, and every stop is recorded. Automatic cleanup stays off until you turn it on for an account.
- **Triage and morning summary**: one sorted view of what needs you, and a summary of what happened while you were away.
- **Parking**: an Instance that hit its usage limit waits, and carries on when the window resets.
- **Worktrees**: run a second Instance in the same project, in its own git worktree, without the two getting in each other's way.
- **History**: search past sessions, replay them read-only, archive them, and choose how long each account keeps them.
- **Backup and restore**: save AI Juggler's own data to one file and restore it, for example on a new Mac.
- **Fleet** (beta): pair your own Macs with a code, then see and manage their Instances from one window.
- **Updates**: AI Juggler checks for a new version when it starts (you can turn this off), shows what changed, and installs only when you choose. Pick Stable or Beta in Settings › About & updates.
- **AI Juggler Nightly**: a separate app with the newest build every night. It installs next to AI Juggler and keeps its own data, so you can try what's coming without touching your everyday setup.
- **Optional Figurehead account**: connect one for end-to-end encrypted cloud backup and for finding your Macs for Fleet. Everything else works without an account.
- **Privacy**: usage statistics are off unless you turn them on, and Settings › Privacy shows exactly what would be sent.
- **Keyboard and zoom**: every screen works with the keyboard alone and at 200% zoom, in light and dark themes.

## Known issues

- Only Claude Code is supported in this beta. Codex and ZCode are planned.
- AI Juggler needs macOS 14 or later.
- Switching is beta. Read what the switch says will carry over before you confirm it.
- Fleet is beta and has had little testing across two Macs so far.
- Crash reports are kept on your Mac in this build. Nothing is sent.
- Connecting a Figurehead account hasn't been tried against the live service yet. If it doesn't connect, everything else in AI Juggler works without an account.
- The app icon is a placeholder.
