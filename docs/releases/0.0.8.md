Your Claude setups now come in as they are: same sign-in, same history, nothing copied unless you ask. One that can't come in no longer holds up the rest.

## New

- **Use your setups where they are.** Bringing in your Claude setups now uses each one in place, by default. Your Claude app and AI Juggler share one sign-in, one history and one set of settings, and AI Juggler adds nothing of its own to your files. Setups that are signed in stay signed in. **Make separate copies** is right beside it if you want an isolated sandbox. Each account in Accounts says which it is.
- **Use the original instead.** If you brought accounts in as copies before, each copy's card in Accounts can switch it to the folder it came from. The copy stays on disk; nothing is deleted.
- **You decide when some don't come in.** If one setup can't be brought in, the others still are. You can **Continue** with those, **Try again** for the ones that failed, or use a failed copy **where it is instead**. Close or Esc while bringing them in means **Stop**: the one in progress finishes and the rest wait.

## Improved

- **Home is also the morning triage.** Sessions parked until a reset have their own section, with Resume once the reset arrives. "Pick up where you left off" lists what ended this week that you haven't marked reviewed. J/K or the arrow keys move between rows, Enter opens, and A runs the row's action. Triage and Overview, which repeated Home, are gone from More.
- **Adding an account is short.** Name it and sign in, or pick a folder to bring in. Add account asks which tool only when more than one is installed.
- **Cleanup** says what it is in one line, and keeps its switches behind ⋯.
- In a window narrower than 900 px, the session list folds to its icons by itself and comes back when the window is wider.
- The app says "tool" where it said "Provider".

## Fixed

- **Measured quota from Claude's status line now works.** It never ran on a real Mac: the path contains a space ("Application Support") and wasn't quoted. Existing installs are repaired, and your own status line still shows.
- Bringing in a setup that has a linked folder (such as a skill linked from elsewhere) no longer fails. The link is kept as a link.
- Everything you can click shows the pointing hand.
- The bring-in screen can always be closed.
- A launch setting that picks a model no longer overwrites the account's `settings.json`, which used to drop its hooks and permissions.
- Started from a terminal that exports an Anthropic API key, AI Juggler ran every account on that key. Each account now signs in as itself.
- A switch arriving from a paired Mac can't write outside the target account's folder or replace its files (Fleet is beta).

## Known issues

- **Going back to 0.0.7 isn't possible yet.** 0.0.8 updates AI Juggler's own database, and 0.0.7 can't open it afterwards. Your Claude setups, sessions and history in your Claude folders aren't affected. If you need 0.0.7 again, ask in [Discussions](https://github.com/figurehead-ai/AI-juggler/discussions) first. From the next update, AI Juggler keeps a copy from before each upgrade, so you can always go back.
