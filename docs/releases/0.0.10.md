Codex setups can now be used where they are, just like your Claude ones, and opening AI Juggler twice no longer touches its data.

## Improved

- **Codex, where it is.** When you add your Codex setups, you choose: use each one where it is, as when you run Codex yourself, or let AI Juggler make its own copy. Using it where it is comes first. AI Juggler adds nothing of its own to your Codex folder. A Codex copy can switch to its original from Accounts. If a copy fails, you can use the setup where it is instead.

## Fixed

- **Opening AI Juggler while it's already open.** The open one comes forward, and the second launch leaves AI Juggler's data alone. If the open one isn't answering, a message says how to quit it, or force it to quit, before you open AI Juggler again.
