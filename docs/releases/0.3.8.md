AI Juggler 0.3.8 recovers an app or profile that lost its window when macOS restarted its display.

## Fixed

- **An app that lost its window can be restarted from AI Juggler.** When macOS restarts its display (its window server; you see the login screen again, or the screen flashes and every window is gone), an app can keep running with no window, out of reach of the Dock and ⌘-Tab. Open used to try to bring it forward and say it "isn't open now", and starting it again would have put a second copy on the same profile. Now Settings › Desktop apps and Home say **Lost its window when macOS restarted its display**, and Open, Go to it and Open in {app} ask "Restart it?". **Restart** quits it the way ⌘Q does, waits for it to end, and starts it again on the same profile and the same account. Restarting stops anything still working in it, so it only happens when you click Restart. If it doesn't quit within 10 seconds, AI Juggler says so and starts nothing; quit it in Activity Monitor, then start it from AI Juggler.
- **Keep alive restarts it on its own.** An app or profile with Keep alive on that lost its window counts as stopped: AI Juggler quits it and starts it again, with Keep alive's usual limits (at most 3 times in 10 minutes, never while the screen is locked, in AI Juggler's first minute or while it quits). It waits while a session in that app is still working, and its notice says the app lost its window and was restarted.
- **A switch to Desktop won't hand a session to a profile that lost its window.** It says what happened and where to restart it, then you can switch again.

## How AI Juggler tells

An app counts as having lost its window only when macOS no longer lists it among the running apps **and** it started before your current login session. An app started since you logged in is never treated this way, and if AI Juggler can't read either fact it assumes the app is fine.

## Known issues

- Only on macOS. Windows and Linux builds don't check for this yet.
