AI Juggler 0.3.6 makes Usage's numbers mean what they say: costs priced the way each call is billed, tokens counted the same way for every tool, Codex's sessions counted as conversations, and a limit's reading kept apart from any estimate.

## Fixed

- **Estimated costs are priced the way each call is billed.** A Codex call with more than 272K tokens of input pays OpenAI's long-context rates (about twice the input price and one and a half times the output price), and Claude's 1-hour cache writes cost twice the input price instead of the 5-minute rate. Both were priced at the standard rate, so costs read low: on a heavily used Mac by about 5% overall and 18% for Codex. Claude Haiku 5.5 now has its price (higher for prompts over 100,000 tokens), and fast mode and US-only inference are priced at their own rates when Claude Code uses them. After the update AI Juggler reads your history once more to price every call, so the figures settle within a few minutes.
- **Usage counts new tokens for every tool.** The first tile is now **New tokens**: input, cache writes, output and reasoning, with what was re-read from the cache shown under it. "Tokens in and out" left out the cache writes that carry nearly all of Claude's new input, and Codex's reasoning, so Claude looked about five times smaller than its real use. The charts, the splits by tool, model and project, the top lists and the session pages count the same way.
- **Codex: automatic reviews and subagents count under their conversation.** Each automatic code review and each subagent runs in a thread of its own, and every one was counted as a session (on one Mac, 2,285 of 2,779). Their tokens and cost now count under the conversation that started them, and a conversation's page includes them.
- **A limit shows the tool's own reading, and when it was taken.** The percentage beside "Measured" used to add a projection of the use since the reading. It now is the reading, "as of" its time, and when you've worked since, an estimate of what's left now is shown apart and called an estimate.

## Known issues

- Codex's `codex-auto-review` model has no published list price, so its tokens aren't in the estimated cost, and the cost tile says "at least".
- History whose files are no longer on your Mac keeps the price it had, at the standard rate.
