AI Juggler 0.3.9 switches long conversations, says how much disk space a switch uses before it starts, shows how far it has got, and says why one stopped.

## Fixed

- **Long conversations switch.** A conversation long enough that its history passed about 500 MB (common for a Claude app conversation used for weeks) couldn't be switched: every try ended in "Something went wrong… Try again". Now a conversation of any size is copied whole, and quickly: in a test, an 800 MB one switched in about 5 seconds, with its subagents, tool results and file history, and AI Juggler's memory use stays small. Long Codex conversations, compressed or not, switch too.
- **A conversation still open in its app switches.** The Claude app keeps writing to a conversation that's open in it, and AI Juggler took that as the original being changed, so the switch failed after the new session had started. Now it checks that everything it carried is still exactly there in the original; a reply written meanwhile is fine, and stays in the original.
- **A switch says how much disk space it uses, and never fills your disk.** Before you switch, the switch screen says how big the conversation is, how much disk space the copy takes and how much is free. On a Mac the copy is usually a clone, which takes almost no space; switching back to an account that already has the conversation, or a packed Codex conversation, takes its full size. When space would run low it says so and suggests what to free; when there isn't enough, Switch waits until there is, with **Check again**. A switch always leaves at least 4 GB free, checking again as it copies in case something else is filling the disk.
- **You see how far a switch has got.** The switch window shows each step as it happens (copying the conversation, checking the copy, starting the new session, confirming), with a bar and, for a long conversation, the seconds so far.
- **A switch that stops says why.** Instead of "Something went wrong", it says what happened and what to do: the disk is full, AI Juggler wasn't allowed to write to the account's folder, the original is still writing its reply, the conversation is too large, or its file is gone. **Try again** shows only when trying again can work. **Copy details** copies what happened, for a bug report.
- **Sessions started from another sit under it.** In the Claude app, a session you start from another one sits under it in the sidebar. Open now showed them side by side; now they sit under the session that started them, one step in per level, as in the app.

## Known issues

- Switching to another Mac still carries a conversation only up to 512 MB.
