AI Juggler 0.3.1 fixes what was found in 0.3.0, starting with one that could lose your history.

## Fixed

- **Deleting launch settings keeps their sessions.** One click on the bin deleted the launch settings and, with them, every session they had made from History. Now it asks first. The settings stop starting sessions, and the sessions they made stay in History. While one of them is still open, the delete waits and names it.
- **Esc closes New session at once.** An Esc pressed right after ⌘N, before the sheet had focus, did nothing.

## Known issues

- **Going back to 0.3.0 from 0.3.1.** 0.3.1 updates AI Juggler's own data to remember deleted launch settings instead of erasing them. The copy it keeps on the way makes going back possible: Settings › About & updates › Go back to an earlier version. That copy is from before the update, so what changed in 0.3.1 isn't in it. Your Claude and Codex folders aren't affected either way.
