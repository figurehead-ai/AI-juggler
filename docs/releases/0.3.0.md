AI Juggler 0.3.0 makes your projects, pools, tasks and subagents easy to find and worth opening, turns a pool into one live canvas you watch and change in place, and makes stopping processes safer.

## New

- **Projects, Pools and Tasks in the header.** The header now reads Home · Projects · Pools · Tasks · Accounts · Usage · History, and the highlighted tab is the page's title. ⌘⇧P opens Projects, ⌘⇧O Pools and ⌘⇧T Tasks.
- **Group the session list with one click.** Under the search, choose App, Project or Account (and Pool once you've made one). Your choice is kept on this Mac.
- **A Projects screen and a page for each project.** Every project shows what needs you, the sessions working, open tasks and this week's tokens. A project's page has Overview, Sessions, Tasks, Pool, Subagents, Usage and History.
- **A Tasks screen across every project.** See your agents' tasks on a board (Ready, In progress, In review, Blocked) or as the plan of epics, stories and tasks, filtered by project, pool or who holds them. Approve, send back or free a task from the card. Agents still write the tasks; tasks are named by their titles everywhere.
- **Pools, one live canvas.** The Pools screen shows each pool with a small live picture of it. A pool's page is a canvas: its members and what they're doing, the messages and tasks passing between them as they happen, and anything waiting on you, answered right on the member that asked. Drag a session in to add it, drag one out to take it out (with Undo), make a member the lead, and change the pool's access level and rules beside it. Arrange the members as you like and the canvas keeps your layout; Tidy up lays it out again.
- **Subagents you can use.** A session's Subagents tab now opens on what's running, then what failed, then everything, with search and the share of the session's tokens they used. Subagents are grouped under the message that started them, with lines showing which subagent started which. Open one to see the task it was given, its result, the tools and files it used, its tokens and estimated cost, and the processes it probably started. A project's page shows the subagents of all its sessions. Even a session with hundreds of subagents opens at once.
- **What the other tool was told.** After a switch to another tool, the last step says in one line what was hidden from it and what was left out, with **Open the handover** to read it. **Save handover…** is on a session's page, next to Continue on another account, and ends with Reveal in Finder.
- **Switch back when there's room again.** When an account you moved work away from has room again, its notice names that work and offers **Switch back…**.

## Improved

- **A session that hits its limit and stays open says so.** A Claude Code session that stops at its usage limit keeps running, so AI Juggler used to notice only once it ended. Now it reads "Limit reached" at once, and Needs you, the limit dialog and the switch list appear while the session is still open.
- **The View menu, redone.** It's a real menu now: every line with an icon and its explanation underneath, and each "Only" choice opening a list instead of a system dropdown.
- **One look for every choice.** Checkboxes, radio buttons and dropdowns look and work the same everywhere. Menus open from their button instead of covering it, and every dialog closes with ×.
- **The stop sheet says what it checked** in one line ("Checked: the process AI Juggler started · its session has ended · stopping frees :5173"), with the details a click away, in plain words throughout. The report links to Cleanup history.
- **The folded session list keeps a way to its grouping.** Below 1100 pixels wide the list folds to icons; the View button at its top opens the list, with its grouping switch and View, as ⌘B does.
- **Your Macs:** "No access" can be chosen for a paired Mac, a Stop on another Mac waits 5 seconds with Undo as a Stop on this Mac does, and a paired Mac's processes point you to Processes › Your other Macs.

## Fixed

- **A wider stop checks every process before it stops it.** "Whole session tree" could stop a process whose number had since been reused by another program. Each process is now checked like the one you clicked and left alone if it isn't the same. "This process and its helpers" stops only the helpers AI Juggler started.
- **The unsaved-work warning shows for a single process.** Stopping an MCP server, a shell or a database now warns that it may hold unsaved work even when it is alone, which is always so for another app's leftover.
- **Saved handovers are yours only.** Their folder and files could be read by other users of the Mac. A handover made while you chose to keep a key in no longer says that key was hidden.

## Known issues

- **Subagents of Kimi Code, Grok Build and OpenCode** are listed, but their prompts, results and usage aren't shown yet, and their conversations can't be opened.
- **One subagent can't be stopped from AI Juggler.** In Claude Code: /tasks, select it, press x.
- **Processes a subagent started are matched from the commands it ran,** so they're marked "probably".
- **Going back to 0.2.0 or earlier from 0.3.** 0.3 updates AI Juggler's own data. The copy it keeps on the way makes going back possible: Settings › About & updates › Go back to an earlier version. Pool layouts from 0.3 aren't in that copy. Your Claude and Codex folders aren't affected either way.
