AI Juggler 0.1.6 lets you watch and handle your sessions without leaving it, measures every Claude account's limits, and makes every screen bigger, clearer and more visual.

## New

- **Dashboards.** Usage, each project, each profile and each account now show headline numbers with trends, usage over time by account, profile, tool, model or project, pie charts, limit meters with their reset times and history, and an activity heatmap. Pick 24 hours, 7, 30 or 90 days, or everything. Each chart can also be shown as a table.
- **Real app icons.** Every row, tile and header shows the app's own icon as it's installed on your Mac, with the profile as a small dot beside it. No icon is copied or sent anywhere.
- **Claude limits for every account.** AI Juggler now reads the Claude app's own limit readings, the limit hits Claude Code records and Claude Code's last /usage result. When a profile's reading is old and the profile is in use, it runs Claude Code's own /usage check there, at most every 15 minutes; no message goes to Claude. Each reading says where it came from and how old it is, and a 5-hour reading from before its window reset shows as history. Each profile's page can turn the check off.
- **A real conversation on the session page.** Up to 30 turns, newest at the bottom, with your turns on the right and the agent's on the left, formatted text and code, the commands it ran folded into one line, and "Show earlier" for more. It follows a working session live, and when a session asks you something, the question sits in a card at the bottom.

## Improved

- **Everything opens here first.** A click on any item in the left list opens its page in AI Juggler: a session, its project, a profile or the "Also" list. ↗ or ⌘↩ goes to the app itself.
- **Go to it brings the window forward.** It now hands focus over the way macOS expects, checks that the app really came to the front, and says so plainly when macOS didn't.
- **Readable everywhere.** Larger text throughout (nothing under 12 px), clearer secondary text, one page layout that uses wide windows well, one set of status words, and plain language in place of jargon.
- **Usage in the top bar.** The top bar now reads Home, Accounts, Usage, History and Settings; Privacy lives in Settings, and the tool page is part of Settings › Accounts.
- **History by day.** Sessions are grouped under Today, Yesterday and earlier dates, with filters for project and profile, and an untitled session is named by what you last asked it.
- **This Mac explains memory pressure.** It shows the whole Mac's memory (AI tools, other apps, what macOS keeps compressed and what's free), names the other apps using the most, and says what memory pressure means.
- **Fast with many sessions.** Pages show something at once and long lists load a page at a time, so AI Juggler stays quick with hundreds of sessions.

## Fixed

- **The spinning wheel.** Opening a session or project page could freeze AI Juggler for seconds. All of its work now runs off the window's thread.
- **Session page.** The page no longer reloads by itself, and its Overview and Usage tabs no longer blink in and out.
- **Codex status and titles.** A Codex thread that is working shows Working instead of Idle, and a block the app inserts is never used as its title.
- **Menus.** A row's menu opens at the pointer, not further down the window.
