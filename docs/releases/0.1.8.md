AI Juggler 0.1.8 lets you move any session to another account or tool, even one that's still open in its app, and gets limits, sizes and prices right.

## Improved

- **Continue any session on another account or tool.** A session open in the Claude app, in ChatGPT's Codex or in a terminal now offers every signed-in profile of every tool, with Codex next to Claude and the other way round. One click opens the switch screen and its checks. The original stays open in its app, untouched: AI Juggler only reads its conversation and continues it as a new session.
- **Claude's weekly limit.** An account whose only weekly limit is on one model now shows it, for example "This week · Fable only", instead of saying it has no weekly limit.
- **The Codex app on This Mac.** Codex running in the ChatGPT app has its own group, with its processes, next to the Claude app's. A `claude` you started in a terminal is listed too.
- **Prices and details.** The sheet now leads with what your usage would cost at list prices and what you pay. Sessions are named by their title and project, a session's models on one day share one row, and the table fits without scrolling sideways.
- **Sizes read the way your Mac reads them.** Files and disks are counted as Finder counts them, and memory as macOS does, so a 96 GB Mac reads 96 GB.

## Fixed

- **Sessions that ended stayed "running".** For sessions AI Juggler runs, an ended session wasn't recorded once AI Juggler had been quiet for a moment, so it wasn't restarted after a crash and you weren't told it finished. It's recorded every time now.
- **Background updates could stop.** One error could stop This Mac's figures, the cards and other live updates until AI Juggler restarted. Each update now carries on.
- **The Prices total.** It showed one model's cost for one day, and nothing at all once any model had no list price. It now adds up every priced model and names the ones it leaves out.
- **Counts like "1000k".** A count just under a million now reads 1.0M.
- **Old prices are flagged.** The warning that the price table is over 30 days old can now show.
- **The replay's lineage.** It names the tool, "Claude Code", instead of its id.
