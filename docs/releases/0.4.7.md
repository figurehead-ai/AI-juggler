AI Juggler 0.4.7 fixes what 0.4.6's beta soak and its last release candidate found: pools that ask and answer in one obvious place, honest limits and names, and Processes that let go of what you stopped.

## Improved

- **One press approves a held message.** When a pool asks before passing a message on, "Allow and deliver" now approves it and wakes the session it's for at once. "Allow, deliver later" leaves it for that session's next turn, and Deny is as before. Before, it took Allow once and then Deliver.
- **A pool's limit on agent messages counts the whole back-and-forth.** A pool set to ask after two agent messages in a row now asks at the third, even when an agent answers in a new thread. Before, a new thread started the count again, so four went through.
- **A Reviewer's send-back reaches the writer.** When a Reviewer sends a task back with a note, the session holding the task gets the note through the pool's messages, so the writer and reviewer finish the loop without you. Before, only the task's history had it.

## Fixed

### Pools

- **Pause and Stop end a waiting ask.** Pausing or stopping a thread that waits at its limit takes the ask off Home, and nothing reopens the thread but Resume.
- **The limit reads "2 of 2"** everywhere it shows ("2 of 2 agent messages in a row without you; one more waits for you"), and it holds for every member, including a Claude app session and a terminal AI Juggler can't type into.
- **A refused message says which rule refused it** ("Member to Member messages are blocked in this pool"), and a session that names a pool it isn't in is told which pool holds it and how to reach the other.
- **An agent's task change that waits on you can be answered where it's named.** The task's own page and the pool's Tasks now show the same Allow and Deny card as Home, and the pool's "2 need you" opens them. Before, the rail and the bell led to a page that said "waits on your OK" with no way to answer.
- **Deleting a pool is one obvious step.** A pool's page has a ⋯ with Rename and Delete in its header, and each card's ⋯ is always in sight. The side panel and the List view scroll with the wheel.
- **The Pools grid holds still** when you make a pool: the new one goes after the others.
- **Roles from the List view.** Each member's row in the List view sets its role, and the rule between any two members, as its card on the diagram does. New pool names the Reviewer a template promises.
- **The everyone setting says what it changes.** "Everyone in web, now and new, is: Reviewer · 59 sessions now" replaces "New sessions in web join as", which changed the sessions already in, too.
- **Pool setup says who joins now and who later**, says "it joins" only when a new session would, and keeps "Use a template" until setup is done.
- **A session held by a sealed pool is counted and named** in its other pool's header, Who's in and diagram, with no lone "No lead" card, and the Pools cards no longer draw it.
- **Cards and names read as the diagram does:** an Account member is "Personal", not "An Account"; a told-apart project pool is "web (in lab)" in Settings › Agent access; a renamed session is named by its new name everywhere, not its name and old title together.
- **A new pool's diagram is centred** when none of its sessions is open, not a column at the right edge, and a pool's Tasks and Messages open on a skeleton at once.
- **The setup's steps can be taken from its left rail**, and a step not open yet says after which one it opens.

### Sessions and switching

- **A switched session keeps its name and says where it came from.** After Continue in another app, the new session goes by the conversation it carried, and its header says "continues from …", a door to the original. Before, it read "web · started 9:40 AM".
- **New session starts where you are.** It opens in the project of the page you're on, else where you last started one, on the App used there; Recent holds its order, and the sheet no longer shifts as it fills.
- **"Suggested" is never on an App that can't run now.** An App out of room keeps its warning and loses the mark; the App with room is the one suggested.
- **Continue in another app… says why when it can't.** An Account's "What this affects" shows the reason beside a greyed Continue, and Home's and the bell's cards agree with it.
- **The Stop toast names the session** ("Stopping p1-writer."), then where it runs.
- **Continue in another app… fits the window** down to 1024×680, with its choices scrolling and Cancel in sight.
- **A session that stopped with an error says why** on its page where its ending is known ("Claude Code exited with code 1, at 8:35 AM.").
- **Open in terminal is one row** in the sidebar and in Home's last 12 hours.
- **Recovery:** a session run without a terminal whose conversation file was deleted starts again after a restart and says why; work switched back from your other Mac keeps the name you gave it there; a look already running when a session ends no longer shows it as open again.

### Limits, privacy and this Mac

- **Limits read exactly.** A driven Claude session's limits read the tool's own fraction as it is, so a window past its cap reads as all used, and a week on one model reads "Weekly (Opus)".
- **"Signed in as" never shows your email in full.** A Claude organisation named after its email is masked too.
- **Processes let go of what you stopped.** A left-behind process you stop leaves the list at once, and one from before AI Juggler last started leaves when it ends. Before, it stayed listed until a restart.
- **This Mac says what "room" means**: free memory, swap, the processor, heat and disk, and when each turns tight.
- **Date fields are never cut** ("Choose a da").
