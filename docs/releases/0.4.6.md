AI Juggler 0.4.6 is the candidate for the first Stable: pools you set up in plain words, Claude app sessions as full pool members, and a long round of fixes.

## New

- **Set up a pool one plain step at a time.** Pools › New pool, and New pool for web… on a project's Pools tab, open one guided setup: where, who's in, who leads, who builds and who checks, who may message whom, and what they may do on their own, each with a line on why and a live drawing of the team beside it. Every choice reads back as a sentence that says whom it applies to, and from Who's in on, Make pool now fills in the rest with the defaults. Review says first whether the pool can work, and what to tell its lead, with Copy.
- **A pool always has a way to a lead.** Who leads offers a session open now, an App's next session ("It takes charge as soon as one of its sessions starts in web") or No lead for now. Before, a pool with Apps in it and no session open had nothing to pick. On the diagram, Start one now opens New session on that App in the pool's project, and the session you start leads.
- **What sessions may do on their own, in words.** The choices are now Look only, Work together, and Work together and wake each other (they were Read, Work together and Full), each with what it does and an example, and the same on New pool, the pool's page and Settings › Agent access. A member can follow the pool (Same as the pool) or have its own choice, near the top of its panel. Who may message whom starts from three ways: Everyone talks freely, Members ask you first, or Members go through the lead.
- **Team tools are asked once, in plain words.** AI Juggler asks once whether the sessions in your pools may message each other and share notes and tasks, saying what it gives, that it costs nothing extra and that it changes nothing in your apps. After Not now, a pool says so, with Turn on team tools; you can switch them off for a project or an app in Settings › Agent access.
- **A new pool's page shows what's left to set up.** Until a session joins, its diagram draws the team you planned, and the panel at the right is its checklist ("Setting up · 5 of 7"), each step with its value and a Change. Once a session has joined and every step is done, or with Hide setup, the panel is the pool's calm live view: Now, Tasks, Lead, On their own, Messages this week and Live. Edit setup brings the checklist back.
- **Keep it apart… is one sheet.** On a project's menu or its page's Agents card, it says in plain words what keeping the project apart does and doesn't do; before, it opened New pool's nine steps. Keep apart makes the sealed pool at once, named after the project ("web · kept apart"), and Customise… opens New pool to change it first. The project's Agents card says whether it's kept apart ("Kept apart: no · its sessions can be in your other pools too").
- **Claude app sessions can work in a pool.** A session in a Claude app profile's Code tab now reads and sends the pool's messages, notes and tasks under the pool's rules, as a terminal session does, through AI Juggler's connector, which you add to that profile once. Add says first in one sentence what it writes, with each file under Show the files, and nothing is written until you click Add to Claude · Work; Remove takes out exactly that, and asks first if you changed one of its files. AI Juggler's plugin for Claude Code now comes with every release, for adding it by hand (Show me how to add it by hand).
- **Each desktop member says how a message reaches it.** A connected member's card says Wakes on new messages or Reads messages on its next turn, and AI Juggler never types into the app's window. A session whose app has no connector is Watched, and its card says why, with Add. The Codex app's threads can't take the connector yet: they stay Watched and say so.
- **A calm diagram, with every card a button.** Logos rest muted, a need is one mark on its node, two members who wrote both ways share one line with an arrowhead at each end, and the lead is tied to each member that hasn't written yet. A pool never laid out opens tidy, and a session's card leads with its own title and is a button VoiceOver can press. Add a member at the top left replaces the list that was always open, and Take out of the pool now waits a moment with Undo, as Stop does.
- **Each Pools card draws its team.** The lead is on top and the members show as their tools' logos, with their open sessions counted. Before, each card's picture was a near-empty dotted box.
- **The switches you're offered are tested ones.** AI Juggler now offers a switch only once it has been tried with the real tools: between two Claude accounts (Copy and Move to account) and between Claude Code and Codex. Codex to Codex, switches through another service, switches to another of your Macs and switches with the other tools are still being tested: turn on Settings › Switching › Beta › Show switches still in beta to list them, each marked Beta. A tested switch no longer says "Not proven yet", and Accounts' table says Tested or Beta for each.
- **Limits from the sessions AI Juggler runs count for their account.** A Codex session, or Claude Code without a terminal (Beta), tells AI Juggler its account's limits as it works. Each new reading now counts for the account at once, on its runway, its Out mark and its notices, named for where it came from ("from Claude Code's run in AI Juggler"); before, only the session's own panel showed it, which could say 36% used while the account's runway said 100% left. A limit on one model alone shows on the session's own page, never as the account's week.

## Fixed

- **New session says which App and Account it will use, and never changes them unseen.** Picking another folder in New session could quietly switch to the App last used there, which is how work starts on the wrong account. Now one line shows the App and its Account with its room, an App you picked stays when you change folder (the card offers the folder's own: "Use Claude Code CLI · Personal"), and when a suggested App moves with the folder, the card says so ("App changed to … (was …): the only account used here. Undo"). Change app lists the folder's suggestion first.
- **Sessions show where they really run.** A session AI Juggler runs reads as running in AI Juggler, even when AI Juggler was started from a terminal inside another app, such as a Claude app profile's Code tab; it could read as running in that app, and Go to it would have brought that app forward. A conversation you stopped in AI Juggler and then opened in its Claude app profile shows open under that profile while the app runs it, where the profile read "nothing open".
- **Open now lets go of what has ended at once.** A session you stop leaves Open now at once, where its conversation stood as its own "Idle" row for up to 15 seconds. A session picked up after a restart that then ends shows as ended within a second; it stayed Working until AI Juggler restarted. A Claude app session in a pool counts as open exactly while its app runs it.
- **A quit and reopen brings back every session AI Juggler runs without a terminal.** One that hadn't taken its first turn yet came back as a terminal that failed at once, in Needs you; it now comes back as it was. Open in terminal before the first turn now opens it in a terminal, where it failed the same way, and a start that fails says so, never "stopped by you".
- **Names and pins go with your work.** A name you gave a session, and its pin, now carry to wherever its work goes on: a switch to another account or app and back, Resume, Undo, Continue in AI Juggler, Open in terminal, Run in AI Juggler and your other Mac. Before, the session there had no name ("team · started 12:32 PM"). Your name for a session also names its conversation in every list, over the app's own title.
- **Your own messages show in a session AI Juggler runs.** In Claude Code without a terminal, the conversation showed only what the agent did; it now shows what you sent, marked You, once each. A message another session sent shows by its sender's line, not as yours.
- **Deny and stop says so in words.** After Deny and stop, the conversation showed the tool's error code, and the line under the message box kept it after the next good turn. It now says "Stopped: you chose Deny and stop" (or "Stopped: you interrupted it"), other stops are in words too, and the line goes once the next turn runs.
- **Usage says how old a reading is.** After AI Juggler had been closed a while, Home's runway, the app cards and the rail's Out marks showed days-old readings as current. A reading older than an hour now says so ("34% left this week · as of 2 days ago", with "refreshing…" while AI Juggler reads again), and a limit whose reset has passed is never Out. At start, the Claude apps' own readings now come in first, before AI Juggler reads every transcript.
- **No Out mark on an App with room.** When an App moved to another Account while the window was open, its new Account's reading could still count for the old one, so another App there wore Out with 93% left. The marks now follow the move.
- **Home colours room only when it matters.** Home's runway and app cards turned an account amber at 25 % left or less. They now colour it only while one of its sessions is open: amber at 10 % or less, red when it's out.
- **Fewer bursts of CPU while AI Juggler watches your sessions.** With its window shown and ten sessions working, no minute of our test went over 2 % of one CPU core with little else running (median 1.6 %), where the same test without these changes went over it in two minutes of five; covered by another window, its median is under 1 %. It now tells the window about your Mac's processes only when the count it shows moves, and draws Home and each app's icon and badges again only when what they show changes.
- **Move to account says when it can't, and why.** It was greyed with "proven for version 4.x" after the Claude app updated. It now checks the app's records rather than its version, so it works with the Claude app's 5.x versions, and where it can't work for a pair it isn't shown, so Copy is the choice. Where you can clear what's in the way, it says how ("Move becomes available once they finish"), a message still waiting to be sent in the Claude app included.
- **The bell's panel opens above a pool's panels.** On a pool's page, its Tasks panel covered most of the bell's panel, leaving an empty strip while the page around it couldn't be used. It now opens above every page's own panels, whole.
- **Needs you, the rail and the bell agree, at once.** Archiving a session now takes it out of Needs you and the bell at once, from any screen, and unarchiving brings it back; the bell could stay at 1 over its own "You're all caught up" for close to a minute. A need that ends with nothing else changing, such as a sign-in you just finished, now leaves the rail at once, and Home, the rail and the bell read one list.
- **Open in Claude app says what it did, and opens the right session.** On a profile that wasn't running, it said "Starting … the session opens once it's up" and nothing after; it now says what happened once the app is up. A profile just started could open the session it had open last instead, so AI Juggler now sends the session again until the app shows it, or says to pick it in the app's sidebar. A Claude app conversation you ran in AI Juggler and stopped now has Open in Claude app on its page.
- **Messages this week counts every message.** A pool's panel said "None yet" beside three messages in Live: it counted only agents' messages between members on the diagram. It now counts every message the Messages tab lists, yours and those to you included, and says "20+" when there may be more.
- **Agents reach a session or a project's pool by the name you see.** An agent asked to share something "in the web pool" was refused, because the pool's full name is "web · all sessions"; it now reaches a project's own pool by its project's name or its card's words, and when two of its pools answer to the same words, it's told both names. Agents write to a member, or give it a task, by the name the pool lists it under; only a name two members share is refused, naming each.
- **The hop count reads against the pool's own limit.** In a pool set to stop and ask you after 2 messages, a thread read "2 of 6 agent messages in a row without you", and so did the agents. The count now reads against the pool's own Stop and ask you after ("2 of 2"), in the thread, Needs you and the agents' words.
- **Live quotes each reply.** A pool's Live line quoted every message by its thread's subject, so a reply read as the message it answered. It now quotes a reply by its own first line, and a subject made from a message is cut at a word and ends in "…", never mid-word.
- **Each page keeps its own thread.** Going from one session's page to another's kept the thread the first page had open in its Messages tab, beside a list that didn't hold it. Each session's and pool's Messages tab now shows a thread from its own list.
- **The Pools cards stay put while you look.** The Pools screen sorted its cards by their latest activity on every update, so a click could land on a pool that had just moved under the pointer. The cards now stay where they are while you're on the screen, and a pool made meanwhile goes at the end.
- **An ended Lead keeps its name.** Once the Lead's session ended, its Pools card named it by a string of letters and numbers; it now names it by its title, as the diagram does. Who leads lists that lead, chosen, and says it has ended, where it said No lead for now.
- **A project's own pool has one name everywhere.** Its card read "web (project pool)", a name the agents couldn't use. Its card and window title now say its one name ("web · all sessions"), as its page, a session's Pools tab and the agents do, with "Project pool" beside it.
- **A big pool draws its open sessions.** A pool with 88 sessions, 3 of them open, said "Nothing open now" and "No one has joined yet" beside its header's "3 open". Its diagram now draws the open sessions and its Now counts them as the header does, and a project's own pool no longer says "50 finished · 500 ended" for 500 ended sessions.
- **A message to the whole pool goes to who's there.** It now goes to the members open now, never to one that has ended. A member that comes back gets what it missed, the newest 50, and is told once how many earlier ones there were.
- **Only real Apps are offered.** New session, a project's start lists, a task's Start a session, the switch dialog and the question when an app's sign-in changed offered "Claude Code CLI · Work Max" for a Claude app profile's folder: an App that doesn't exist. They now offer only real Apps, and a switch into that profile names it as what it is ("Claude app · Work Max").
- **Pick up where you left off counts every session to review.** Home counted at most 200 ("Show all 200", "Mark all 200 reviewed") and marked only those. It now counts, shows and marks every session to review from the last 7 days, a page at a time, and Undo puts back just that batch.
- **Filter menus count with your other filters.** On a project's History tab, with one App chosen, the Tool menu still offered a tool with nothing there. Every list's filter menus, History's too, now count each choice with your other filters applied and leave out what would show nothing.
- **Counts and times say what's true.** An Account's and This Mac's Apps counted only the newest few hundred sessions ("67" beside an App with 333); they now say each App's real count. An App's Projects said "20773 d ago" for a project only other apps had worked in, and Usage's Sessions tile left out AI Juggler's own sessions whose tool keeps no token counts; both are right now.
- **Two sign-ins never read alike.** OpenCode's and Kimi Code's sign-ins both read "default needs its sign-in" in Needs you, the rail and the bell; one that shares its name with another now names its whole App ("OpenCode CLI · default"), and VoiceOver hears each row's App. The Sign in terminal's footer names the tool you're signing in to, not Anthropic.
- **Rename replaces the name.** Rename opened with the cursor after the name, so typing "Acme" over "Work" saved "WorkAcme". It now opens with the name selected, for sessions, projects, Apps, Accounts and pools.
- **A session's page names where it runs and its real model.** A session AI Juggler runs without a terminal said "AI Juggler's terminal" and that no model was reported for its first minutes, beside a card that named its model; it now says it runs in AI Juggler, with the model its card shows. After Claude Code hit a limit, a session's page could show a placeholder for its model; it keeps the real one.
- **An ended session's page never opens on a blank terminal.** It showed a black pane for about a second while it checked what was kept. It now shows a placeholder at once, and opening it again shows what it kept straight away.
- **Names, times and paths fit their room.** An Account card's name was cut short ("w…@exam…") with room for it on its own line; it now takes the room it needs, and what follows wraps under it. A rail row's time stays whole beside a long state word ("Ended with AI Juggler"), and a folder path in a sentence breaks only after a "/". A CLI App's Launch settings tab no longer scrolls sideways.
- **The tab you choose lights at once.** A page's tabs, the top bar's sections and segmented choices could keep the old choice lit over the new page for a moment. They now light the one you chose at once.
- **VoiceOver can open every fold.** Go back to an earlier version in Settings › About & updates, every "What does this mean?", Continue in another app's list, This Mac's folds and fifteen more didn't open with VoiceOver's press. Each is now a button it opens.
- **A dialog or menu keeps what's behind it out of reach.** An open dialog only dimmed the window, so a VoiceOver press could still reach Home's Start behind New session. Now nothing behind a dialog, menu or popover takes a press or the keyboard while it's open, and closing it gives the keyboard back to what opened it.
- **Keyboard and contrast fixes.** A held Stop keeps the keyboard's focus instead of dropping it to the top of the page, and Esc in New project's Add an app goes back to New project with your folder kept, where it closed both. Danger buttons have enough contrast on every surface in both themes, and Processes' rows show the pointing hand, as rows you can click do.

## Known issues

- **Going back to 0.4.5.** 0.4.6 updates AI Juggler's own data. The copy it keeps on the way makes going back possible: Settings › About & updates › Go back to an earlier version. That copy is from before the update, so what changed in 0.4.6, such as a pool you made, isn't in it. Going back doesn't touch your Claude and Codex folders, so first remove AI Juggler's connector from any Claude app profile you added it to (Remove, on that app's panel in a pool): 0.4.5 can't.
- A Claude app profile and a Claude Code CLI App that use one folder but are signed in to two different accounts show both Apps on the account that ran last, so one can show the wrong Account and be marked Out while it has room. In 0.5 each App keeps its own Account.
- The Floor is on macOS only. Windows gets it in 0.6 and Linux in 0.7.
