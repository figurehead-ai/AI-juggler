AI Juggler 0.1.9 makes switching to another account clear at every step, says everything in plain words, and fixes what the 0.1.8 walk-through found.

## New

- **A session's History tab.** A session now has a History tab: what happened to it and when, each switch it took, and a link to the project's switch graph. Once the session has ended, you can replay it from there.
- **Connect a Figurehead account on first run.** Setting up AI Juggler ends with an optional step to connect a Figurehead account, with "Continue without an account" next to it. Nothing is chosen for you, and you can connect later in Settings.

## Improved

- **The switch dialog, step by step.** Every step has Cancel and a close button, and the buttons stay on screen however long the step is. The last step opens with "Ready to switch to…" and up to three lines: what moves, what happens to the original, and anything that needs you. Everything else sits under Details. If a switch can't finish, it says what happened in plain words, nothing moves, and Retry is one click away.
- **Only real risks are flagged.** A conversation that was compacted, or a switch to an account other than the usual one, is no longer shown as a warning. Neither one makes a switch carry less.
- **Keys and passwords are asked about only when it matters.** For a switch to the same tool on this Mac, the conversation is copied as it is and nothing is asked. When another tool will read the conversation, you see what was found, who will read it, and what each choice does, with an example. A "What does this mean?" panel and a link to the guide explain more.
- **What moves, in plain words.** The list of what a switch carries names each thing plainly ("The whole conversation", "Project rules", "Connected tools") and says which account will recreate the task list.
- **Things the other account doesn't have.** Each one says whether it holds up the switch. Anything that comes with the subscription shows the steps to turn it on, with a button to open that account's settings page and Check again. A connected tool that needs a token, such as GitHub, can be set up without it, with the exact command to give that account its own token. AI Juggler never copies a token.
- **Plain words everywhere.** Errors, limit alerts, tool names and statuses read in plain words, never internal names. A limit alert reads like "Mia has 7% left in its 5-hour window, until 9:09 PM", with Hand over and Queue for the reset. The technical details go to a log that Export diagnostics includes, with keys removed.
- **The go-to shortcut** now brings AI Juggler to the front before it opens the palette.
- **Compact rail.** The Compact density now applies to the rail, and folding the rail with ⌘B keeps the density you chose.

## Fixed

- **"The end of its transcript holds no messages."** A long-running session in the Claude app could show no conversation while the app showed one. Its latest messages show now.
- **A key in search results.** A search result could show part of a key from a conversation. Keys are now hidden before the result is cut to fit.
- **The cost tile's "$0".** When nothing could be priced, it now says "No list price" or "No usage yet". When only part could be priced, it shows "≥" the amount it could price.
- **Settings that didn't save.** If a setting can't be saved, the previous value comes back with a message saying why.
- **A folder dropped on New session** now becomes the session's folder.
