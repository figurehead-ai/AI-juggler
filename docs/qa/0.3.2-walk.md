# 0.3.2: a scored hand walk of every element changed

The maintainer, 2026-10-30: "If you put up a component and UI and any element, you are to test and validate every single functionality, not just it's there, but actually test it, manually, click it, measure it, evaluate quality, and score result."

**Setup.** The walk ran on 2026-10-30 in the vite preview of `release/0.3` (port 5193) at 1280×800, in dark and light. A walk page seeded three room notices on accounts with open sessions, plus a due reminder and a waiting one, the way the gallery does. It wasn't committed.

**Timing.** Times run from a real click (or a dispatched key) to the change in the page, read with a DOM observer or timer polling. The credits surfaces have their own walk in [0.3.2-credits-walk.md](0.3.2-credits-walk.md).

**Scores.** 1 to 5 for works, speed, clarity and layout. Anything under 4 was fixed before release.

## Colours

Checked by eye on Home, a session page, Tasks and Processes, in dark and light:
- **Cards stand off the page:** the light page is grey under white cards; the dark surfaces are a visible step apart.
- **Sentences read a step down from headings**, and labels sit dimmer still.
- **The primary button is one solid colour:** New session, Open feat/read-model, Sign in.

**Before release:** the full gallery is re-rendered in the pinned container. The a11y gate measures every level on every surface (axe colour contrast, both themes).

## New session after a launch

The report: the sheet stayed open after a second launch.
- **In the preview,** every launch fails by design ("Preview mode — no backend connected."), and the sheet stays open with that message. That is what was seen.
- **In the app,** a successful launch calls `onLaunched`, which closes the sheet and opens the new session (`App.tsx`, `closeNewSession`).
- **Not walked:** a real launch in the Dev edition. It would start a real session.
