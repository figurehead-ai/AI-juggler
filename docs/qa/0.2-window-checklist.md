# 0.2 at the window: the steps only you can do

The read-only walk of 0.2
([walk 2026-10-28](evidence/walks/2026-10-28-0.2.md)) saw every screen, but
not what needs a click that changes something. This list is that part:
about 15 minutes, in AI Juggler Dev, in a throwaway lab folder, with your
lab accounts. Write what you saw on each blank line; a word or two is
enough.

**Safe by construction**
- AI Juggler Dev from `release/0.2`, never the installed AI Juggler.
- Only the lab profiles **bookauth-luka** and **bookauth-anela** (Claude)
  and your Codex home. Sign in to nothing; press no macOS Allow.
- A new empty folder, `$LAB`. Tiny prompts only ("say hi").
- Every step says how to undo it. Step 10 stops everything you started.

**Before you start (2 min)**

```sh
export LAB="$HOME/lab/aj-walk-0.2"
export DISABLE_AUTOUPDATER=1           # no tool updates itself during the lab
mkdir -p "$LAB" && cd "$LAB" && git init -q && echo lab > README.md
cd ~/Dev/AIJuggler && git switch release/0.2 && just dev
```

In AI Juggler Dev: the three lab profiles are there, and Settings › Agent
access › "Yes, let them" is on (steps 3–5 need the agent tools).

## 2. A real switch, all the way (4 min)

**Do**: New session › folder `$LAB` › **bookauth-luka** › Start in
terminal. Type `say hi`. When it answers, open the session's page ›
**Continue on another account** › **bookauth-anela**.

**Expect, step by step**
1. "Step 1 of 3: Target", "Switch to bookauth-anela", "Same conversation,
   new account", and "Nothing moves until you choose Switch now on the
   last step." **Cancel (Esc)** and × on every step.
2. "Step 2 of 3: What's carried" › "What goes along".
3. "Step 3 of 3: Confirm" › "Ready to switch to bookauth-anela".
   If it lists anything under **Set up on bookauth-anela** with **Show me
   how**: open Show me how, do nothing it says, then press **Check again**.
   Expect "Checking…", then words saying what it found. If nothing is
   listed, write "none".
4. **Switch now**: stages in words (no bare spinner), then "Switched to
   bookauth-anela" and **Go to the new session**. The new session answers
   with what `say hi` was about.
