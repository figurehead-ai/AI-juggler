|---|---|
| Open the long session the maintainer compared with the Claude app | The switcher shows **This session · Its conversation**, then **Started from here · 6**. | Click a session it started | Its pane shows in place with **Back** and **Open its page** (282 ms in the dev build; see the timing below). |
