## The tester's run (Sonnet, as Maya; F1)

- **Firewall:** clean. 14 `floor_drive` commands and 6 reads of its own pictures; no other tool except the report hand-back, which the check now allows.
- **Result:** the goal wasn't reached. The tester saw "1 waiting on you", a crashed desk and an asleep (out) desk. A click on an area or a person only selected it and named it ("… · 1 SESSIONS · WORKSHOP"). Once the Floor was open, the main window was gone.

## What it found (for triage on the candidate)

1. **Not a product bug (checked 2026-11-07):** opening the Floor put the main window off screen at exactly the Floor's frame, and a door changed its page unseen. It happens only when the binary runs outside its .app bundle (the harness build was `--no-bundle`). The bundled Dev build, on the same lab, opens the Floor as its own window and keeps the main window on screen. A change disallowing window tabs (48dd5ba92) didn't help and was reverted (c1c5afbc0); the walk runs a bundled harness build (`floor_harness.py build --app`).
2. **Clarity (fixed, ace8790b0):** the Floor says "1 waiting on you" while Home says "2 sessions and 2 more things need you" (Needs you 4). They count different things, but nothing says so.
3. **Clarity (fixed, ace8790b0):** a selected area's label says the App and "1 SESSIONS · WORKSHOP", not what waits or why ("1 SESSIONS" is also a plural slip).
4. **Readability (fixed, ace8790b0 and 34b7b69bc):** the signs' text is small at the Floor's opening view (the tester zoomed in and saw little more).
