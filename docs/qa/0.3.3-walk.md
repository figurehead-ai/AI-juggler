# 0.3.3: Open starts an app, and Keep alive, walked and scored

The maintainer, 2026-10-31: "Clicking on the open in the app should start the app if its not running", and a per-app setting "like a keep alive". Ruled "Both for 0.3.3" (D-63). This page records the walk: the real system with a throwaway profile, then every new control in the window, clicked and timed. It also lists the combinations the tests cover and what wasn't walked.

**Scores.** 1 to 5 for works, speed, clarity and layout. Anything under 4 was fixed before release.

## On the real system (a throwaway Claude profile)

`cargo test -p juggler-app --test places_real -- --ignored --nocapture`, run 2026-10-31 on the maintainer's Mac. It starts Claude with a new profile folder in a temp directory. Nothing was signed in, and only that instance was signalled. The user's own profiles were never touched. Every lab process was ended before the folder was removed (the test checks).

| Step | What happened | Time | Works | Speed | Clarity | Layout |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Open, profile closed | `open -n -a Claude --args --user-data-dir=<lab>`; "Starting Claude · Lab Claude profile" | 44 ms to answer; its main process seen 18 ms later | 5 | 5 | 5 | — |
| Open, profile running | Brought that instance forward, checked in front; never a second instance (checked 2 s later) | 1.03 s | 5 | 4 | 5 | — |
| Keep alive on, then quit (SIGTERM, what ⌘Q sends) | Seen gone after two missed checks, started again behind the app in front, one instance | 10.2 s from quit to running | 5 | 5 | 5 | — |
| Stop from AI Juggler, then quit | Keep alive paused ("you stopped it in AI Juggler"); not started again in 25 s | — | 5 | 5 | 5 | — |

**Found and fixed on the first run.** A stop made right after Keep alive had started the app was missed. The watch finds a new process at its next look (up to 10 s), so the stop matched no place, and keep alive stayed on. The app only stayed down because the start was still counted as "coming up". `places::stopped_here` now reads the process list when the watch doesn't know the process yet. The second run passed every step.

Open on a running profile takes about a second because Go to it waits until macOS says the app is in front, as it does everywhere (AJ-E2102). It is within the 1.5 s limit.

## In the window (vite preview, 1280×800, light and dark)

The places desktop (`aij.preview.places`): Claude · Work Max and Claude · Personal and ChatGPT running (Work Max kept alive), Claude · Lab kept alive and down, Claude · Old seen 9 days ago. Times run from a real click (or a dispatched event) to the change in the page.

**Found and fixed in the window.**
- **A kept-alive app vanished from Home once started.** Home tiled only apps with open sessions, so a started profile with none left Home. Now a kept-alive app keeps a tile while no session tile shows it.
- **The rail menu's Keep alive had no visible tick.** Screen readers heard "checked"; nothing showed. It now draws its ✓ as the order menu does.
- **"· kept alive" wrapped to a line starting with a dot.** It is a small pill now.
- **"Open in Claude · Personal" cut the session's title short.** The button says "Open in Claude"; the profile is the chip under the title, and the full name is the button's accessible name and tooltip.
- **One app, two names.** Home named a profile by its account ("Work Max"), the new rows by its folder ("Work"). The places now name it as Home does, in the window and in toasts and notices.
- **Where it runs said only "Not open now"** on an ended session. It now says "Not open now · last in Claude", matching the button.

## Combinations, and what covers each

| | Covered by |
|---|---|
| App kind: a Claude profile, the default Claude instance, ChatGPT (one instance), an unknown app, a terminal | `start::tests` (profile and default; `-n` for both), `places::tests` (Codex by its link), `plan()` refuses any kind but a desktop app |
| State: running, quit, killed, a reused pid, never seen since AI Juggler started | `keep::tests` (one miss, two misses), `places_real` (quit), `same_start` on every check, the first-minute settle |
| Setting: off, on, paused (3 stops, a stop here, a failed start), interactive-only account | `nothing_starts_unless_keep_alive_was_turned_on`, `keep_alive_starts_a_stopped_place_behind_then_pauses_after_three`, `a_stop_from_ai_juggler_pauses_keep_alive`, `a_fresh_start_waits_a_minute_and_an_interactive_only_account_pauses` |
| Guard: locked, settling after start or wake, quitting | `nothing_starts_while_the_screen_is_locked`, `never_when_locked_while_settling_after_a_sleep_or_quitting` |
| Safety: moved or swapped bundle, executable outside it, relative folder, writable by others, the window writing the record | `a_place_that_isnt_the_apps_any_more_is_never_started`, `an_app_other_users_can_change_is_never_started`, `the_windows_setting_writer_cant_name_a_program` |
| Entry: Home, Settings, the rail menu, an ended session, a notice | `keep-alive.test.tsx`, the gallery, the walk above |

**Not walked on the real system, and why.**
- The ChatGPT (Codex) app: it runs one instance, and the maintainer's own may be open. Quitting it in a test would quit theirs. Its path is the same launcher without `-n`, and its thread link is covered by a test.
- Sleep, lock and logout: they can't be driven from a test without taking over the computer. The guards are tested on their own, and the lock is read from the system each time a start is due.
