# 0.5.1 usability audit of 0.5.0's features (PAT-USE)

**What this is.** A read-only audit of every user-facing feature AI Juggler 0.5.0 shipped, against the standing rule PAT-USE (`docs/design/DESIGN-SYSTEM.md` §5.11): the six questions, answered for each feature as a newcomer who knows nothing would meet it. Done by Opus, 2026-11-23, on `release/0.5.1` at 3c447e38e.

**How.** From the code (`ui/src/…`, the words files, the backend where a screen's claim depended on it), the task text (E35–E38, E15, E20, E23), the 0.5.0 notes and roadmap, and the captures already in the repo (each one opened): `docs/qa/evidence/AJ-E3804`, `AJ-E3808`, `AJ-E3812`, `AJ-E3816`, `AJ-E3519`, `0.5.0-final-walk`, `0.5.0-rewalk-2` and `ui/tests/visual/__baselines__/linux/`. The app, a browser and Playwright were not run. Several captures were taken on Dev builds labelled v0.4.0 during 0.5's build; where a finding rests on one, its row says "seen in a capture; confirm on the 0.5.1 build".

**Left out on purpose.** Findings already filed as 0.5.1 tasks are not repeated in the table; they are named at the end with what this audit adds to them.

The six questions: (1) who uses it and what its name and icon promise; (2) what a newcomer sees, every term, size, download and choice explained; (3) its real value, in one right place, no duplicate; (4) unavailable or empty: hidden, or a guided setup (D-127); (5) system prompts it can cause (PAT-OSP); (6) what would surprise or confuse a sensible person.

## 1. Bot (E38)

1. **Who, and what the name promises.** Anyone with several agents running who wants to ask "what needs me?" without hunting. A robot icon in the header (⌘J) and the word "Bot" promise an AI assistant that answers (D-144). Tooltip is just "Bot" (`lib/bot-ui-words.ts:15`).
2. **A newcomer sees** a can/can't list and three example questions (`components/bot/BotEmpty.tsx:38-88`; `AJ-E3804/captures/w2-2-panel-empty-model-f03.jpg`). With no AI: "Look things up", four shortcuts and an unnamed "Download · 1.8 GB" (`BotEmpty.tsx:94-145`, `bot-ui-words.ts:59-68`). Already filed as AJ-E3823/E3824.
3. **Value.** It answers questions across all apps and accounts in one place and cites the records it used. Remember/Forget adds a small personal glossary (`AJ-E3808/captures/w2-1-what-bot-remembers.jpg`). Duplicates: its look-ups and shortcuts duplicate Search (filed, AJ-E3824). The Written answers switch has four doors: the empty state, the ⋯ menu, Settings › AI › Jobs and Settings › AI › Models. Its name differs between them (finding 6).
4. **Unavailable.** Today the no-AI state keeps an input box and look-ups. D-144 replaces that with "Set up Bot" only (AJ-E3823).
5. **System prompts.** The Apple helper's first start; the local helper's "(null) differs from previously opened versions" (AJ-E3822); a model download raises no prompt. All are in AJ-E1547's registry list.
6. **Surprises.** The page's tab reads "What Bot remembers 0" over a list holding one note (`w2-1-what-bot-remembers.jpg`; `BotPage.tsx:130` and `BotMemories.tsx:84` read the same hook in two places). "This is wrong" is "kept on this Mac only" and is used for nothing the user can see (`bot-ui-words.ts:88-91`). "What Bot remembers" also shows a "Watches · N" tile (`BotMemories.tsx:116`), so watches read as part of Bot's memory, not as their own feature.

## 2. Watches (AJ-E3814…E3817)

1. **Who.** Someone who wants one specific alert ("tell me when api finishes") without turning on a whole notification kind. "Watch…" with an eye icon reads as a passive alert, and the page says so: "A watch only tells you; it never acts" (`components/watches/watches-words.ts:11`).
2. **A newcomer sees** a well-guided form: four numbered steps, a live preview, a suggested lifetime, quiet hours explained in place, and "Starts watching now" in the footer (`AJ-E3816/captures/w2-1-new-watch-form.jpg`). The empty page offers starter watches drawn from what is open now (`watches-words.ts:120-135`).
3. **Value.** Real value: per-target alerts, while every system notification kind is off by default and only "Watches you set" is on (`crates/juggler-app/src/features/notifications/prefs.rs:67-83`). Placement: it has no header tab. The ways in are the bell's "Watches ›", Settings › Notifications' "Manage watches", a page's Watch…, Bot and ⌘K (`lib/nav.ts:31-34`). That is fine under "prime UI space", but no header tab lights while the page is open (`nav.ts` PAGE_UNDER has no entry for it).
4. **Unavailable or empty.** Empty: a guided empty state with starter watches. Not ready: "Watches aren't ready on this Mac yet…" (`watches-words.ts:27`). "Ask Bot" is hidden without an AI (`screens/WatchesScreen.tsx:59`), as D-144 asks.
5. **System prompts.** A watch alert goes through the system's notifications (`notifications/mod.rs:897-910`), and the watch kind is on by default. So for many people the first watch to fire is AI Juggler's first notification ever, and macOS's notification permission can be asked from background work at an unpredictable moment. That breaks PAT-OSP-3. It belongs to AJ-E1547; see "Already logged".
6. **Surprises.** "Finishes" means "a turn ends and it waits for you" in Watches (`watches-words.ts:299`), but "it ended on its own" in Settings › Notifications (`components/NotificationSettings.tsx:42`). Same word, two events. "Watches 1 of 50" beside the title reads like paging (`watches-words.ts:15`; `w2-2-watch-created.jpg`). "Always" previewing "Ends at midnight tonight" is filed (AJ-E2314 L4).

## 3. Home's feed and the digest (E37, AJ-E3712)

1. **Who.** Everyone, every time they open the app. "Home" promises what is happening now and what happened.
2. **A newcomer sees** a headline ("5 need you · 6 open" or "All clear · 3 finished today"), pinned needs, then the feed with six chips (All, Needs you, Finished, Switches & limits, Pools & tasks, This Mac), an "Everything" picker and a ⋯ menu (`components/HomeFeedFilters.tsx:26-33,46,117-131`; baseline `home-light-comfortable-1280x800.png`). On the right are Your apps and Runway by account. The "Everything" picker is a "show one project, App, pool or Account" scope, but its closed label doesn't say so.
3. **Value.** High: one glance answers "what needs me and what happened". Duplicates: (a) the same finishes show three times: in the feed, in the digest item above them (AJ-E2314 L5 covers the feed's own double) and in the bell's "Earlier" list (`components/NotificationCentre.tsx:1-11`; `0.5.0-rewalk-2/captures/dw1-bell.jpg`). That is two lists of what happened, finding 9. (b) Home's pinned needs repeat the rail's Needs you on the same screen; that is D-118's approved design and is not re-filed.
4. **Empty.** "Quiet so far. When your apps start, finish or ask you something, it shows here." with New session and Open an app (`lib/feed-words.ts:306`). Good.
5. **System prompts.** None of its own. The digest's system notification (off by default) has the same notification-permission path as Watches (AJ-E1547).
6. **Surprises.** Three different marks: "Mark all read" (feed ⋯), "Mark all seen" (the away card, `HomeFeedAway.tsx:101`) and "Mark all reviewed" (Finished, `HomeFeedFilters.tsx:125-128`). Nothing says how read, seen and reviewed differ. The digest calls sessions "agents" ("AI Juggler · Digest · 3 agents", `feed-words.ts:311-314`), and its rows "api · finished" twice can't be told apart (`dw1-home-after-burst.jpg`). The release notes call the "While you were away" card "a digest" while a different 15–30-minute "Digest" item sits in the same feed (`docs/releases/0.5.0.md`, "Home shows what happened").

## 4. Models and Settings › AI (AJ-E3810…E3813)

1. **Who.** People who want Bot's written answers, and the privacy-minded checking what runs where. "AI › Models" promises a choice of AI and its cost in disk and downloads.
2. **A newcomer sees** a recommended card with a reason ("Apple Intelligence is on, so it's already part of this Mac: no download, no disk"), a "Why it fits this Mac" checklist, an honesty line on how models were tested, Installed with a disk total, and "Each job uses" (`AJ-E3812/captures/w2-1-models-apple-f02.jpg`; `screens/settings/ai/models-words.ts`). The Qwen naming and the "model" wording are filed (AJ-E3823).
3. **Value.** Clear, honest and sized. Duplicate: "Each job uses" is the whole of Settings › AI › Jobs and is repeated at the foot of Models, the same rows from the same reads (`screens/settings/ai/JobsPane.tsx:1-4`, `ModelsPane.tsx`).
4. **Unavailable.** "Nothing to recommend" says why per case (`models-words.ts:93-99`). But "Each job uses" lists six jobs, and four of them can't use a model at all in 0.5.0: Turn reviews "Not yet", Feed lines, Digest and Watches "No model passes yet" (`models-words.ts:262-269`, `models-view.ts:19`; `w2-2-jobs-turn-reviews-not-yet.jpg`). The Jobs pane is mostly rows a person can do nothing with, against D-127.
5. **System prompts.** None from the screen itself. "Use it" on Apple's model starts the Apple helper (AJ-E1547, AJ-E3822). Download: none.
6. **Surprises.** The lead promises "The AI that writes AI Juggler's Beta reviews and Bot answers" (`models-words.ts:63`), while reviews are "Not yet". "Attention hints" sits under AI › Jobs as "On. Built from the facts, so it needs no model", with no switch (`ModelsJobs.tsx:31,94-98`). It is code, not AI (`crates/juggler-app/src/features/ai/jobs.rs:18`), and the words "attention hints" appear nowhere else in the app, so nobody can tell what it does. The Bot switch is "Written answers" in Bot (`bot-ui-words.ts:54,75`) and "Bot answers" in Settings (`models-words.ts:38`).

## 5. The AI jobs (turn reviews, attention hints, each job's Beta switch)

1. **Who.** The same people as section 4. "Beta" next to a switch promises "works, still being tested".
2. **A newcomer sees** "Turn reviews · Writes a short review when an agent's turn ends · Not yet", "Attention hints · On", and Beta switches only where a model passes. Each job's line says what it does.
3. **Value.** Only Bot answers is real in 0.5.0. Turn reviews aren't built (AJ-E3820; release notes, Known issues). The other three keep their plain versions.
4. **Unavailable.** Shown as "Not yet" rather than hidden (finding 3).
5. **System prompts.** None beyond the helper's, as in section 4.
6. **Surprises.** An "AI jobs" list that is mostly "not yet" makes the feature read as unfinished. Settings › Switching › Beta ("Show switches still in beta", `screens/settings/BetaSwitchesSection.tsx`) is a different Beta switch with the same word. That is acceptable because it is clearly scoped to switching.

## 6. The Floor (E35)

1. **Who.** People who want an ambient view of their agents at work. A ghost cube icon with a chevron, aria and tooltip "The Floor (⌘⇧M)" (`components/FloorButton.tsx:35-50`). The name doesn't say what it is: nothing tells a newcomer it is a live 3D office of their Apps.
2. **A newcomer sees** a pixel-art building, "BUILDING · FLOOR 3 · FLOOR 2 · FLOOR 1" tabs and a "VIEW" menu, with keyboard hints at the foot (`docs/qa/evidence/AJ-E3519/dev-building-day.png`). A floor is a page of up to ten Apps (E35), but on screen it reads like a level of importance or an account.
3. **Value.** An at-a-glance indicator ("the main purpose of everything is to be an indicator", D-67), in its own window, with an accessible list twin (the Floor list). It doesn't duplicate a surface; it is another way to see Open now.
4. **Unavailable.** Where it can't run, a click gives a warning toast saying why (`FloorButton.tsx:18-22`; `features/floor/window.rs:363-365`). On macOS, its only platform, that path is for failures, not a standing state.
5. **System prompts.** None (its own window, app icons read at run time, a global shortcut).
6. **Surprises.** The unlabelled cube between the bell and the avatar. The split button's chevron looks like the avatar's menu arrow (the 0.4.1 finding the D-118 quiet look reopened).

## 7. Processes and its port map

1. **Who.** Anyone whose agents start dev servers and leave them behind. Reached from the header's monitor chip and "N to clean up"; "Processes" promises what runs, what started it and what it left.
2. **A newcomer sees** four count tiles (to clean up, didn't stop, waiting for their parent, running), tabs (Needs attention, Everything running, Ended, Ports, Cleanup history), a definition line for the selected state, and a tree with "Left behind" and "probably a dev server" (`processes-light-comfortable-1280x800.png`; `screens/processes/Toolbar.tsx:36-42`). The port map leads with the port, then the process, then the session, and a process AI Juggler didn't start is "Not from AI Juggler, so it is only shown" (`components/PortMap.tsx:17-22,68`).
3. **Value.** High and in one place. A session's own Processes tab embeds the same view without Ports and Needs attention (`Toolbar.tsx:49`). That is a scope, not a duplicate.
4. **Empty.** "0 to clean up" (`0.5.0-final-walk.md` C). Fine.
5. **System prompts.** None: reading process tables and ports, and stopping processes, raise no macOS dialog.
6. **Surprises.** The tab says "Needs attention" where every other surface says "Needs you" (`Toolbar.tsx:37`).

## 8. Calm attention: the bell, Needs you, the rail, the header (E36)

1. **Who.** Everyone. Colour now means "needs you" (D-117). The bell promises notifications. "Needs you" promises decisions waiting.
2. **A newcomer sees** the rail's "NEEDS YOU ✕ 5" with rows, "OPEN NOW 3 · Your order", and App / Project / Account lenses (Pool too once pools exist). The bell shows a count badge, the header a "Plenty of room" chip, and on one capture "1 account low" beside it (`0.5.0-rewalk-2/captures/k3-session-f3.jpg`).
3. **Value.** One count of what needs you, read from one place (AJ-E3602). The bell's Needs action mirrors Needs you by design. But the bell's "Earlier" (finishes, resets, crashes, 30 days, `NotificationCentre.tsx:1-11`) is a second history beside Home's feed, which 0.5 made the place for what happened.
4. **Empty.** "You're all caught up." and "Nothing needs you right now." (`NotificationCentre.tsx:163-180`). Good.
5. **System prompts.** None from the surfaces themselves.
6. **Surprises.** (a) The bell's heading is "Needs action" (`NotificationCentre.tsx:170`) while the rail, Home and the count say "Needs you". (b) The level mark beside the count reads "✕ 5", like "times five" or "close", and in another capture it is a "⚠ 1" (`p11-poolA.jpg`). The legend says level 2 is "an amber dot" (`components/LegendLevels.tsx:12-13`). (c) The header's "Plenty of room" is This Mac's memory and disk (`crates/juggler-app/src/machine_health.rs:6`), but "room" is the app's word for an account's usage left ("Room again", "5% of its 5-hour window"). Side by side, "Plenty of room · 1 account low" reads as a contradiction (`k3-session-f3.jpg`).

## 9. Unlink this computer (Settings › Advanced › Figurehead & Cloud)

1. **Who.** The few who linked the optional Figurehead account and want to stop. "Unlink this computer…" promises ending the link without losing local data.
2. **A newcomer sees** a ghost button and a confirm sheet: "Cloud features stop on this Mac and its sign-in is removed. Everything stored on this Mac stays as it is, and backups already on figurehead.ai stay there until you delete them." (`components/FigureheadCloudParts.tsx:285-312`). Clear about consequences. "Figurehead" itself isn't explained on the section: the footer says only "Optional. Everything in AI Juggler works without it" (`screens/settings/AdvancedPane.tsx:41-46`).
3. **Value.** Necessary, and in the one right place, beside "Delete cloud data on figurehead.ai".
4. **Unavailable.** Shown only when connected. The connected list includes a toggle for a feature that "Arrives in an upcoming beta build" (`FigureheadCloudParts.tsx:26-28`), a control that can't work yet (D-127).
5. **System prompts.** The device token is AI Juggler's own Keychain item (`crates/juggler-account/src/secrets.rs:1-3`). Deleting it, or reading it after an update that changed the app's signature, can raise macOS's "wants to use your confidential information stored in … your keychain" dialog. AJ-E1547's expected list doesn't name Keychain (finding 16).
6. **Surprises.** The button and title say "this computer" (`0c5c38796`), while the sheet's body, the section ("Cloud features on this Mac") and the link step ("Approve this Mac") say "Mac". Two words for one thing in one sheet.

## 10. The switch flows' 0.5 changes (D-142, D-143)

1. **Who.** Anyone whose account runs low. The product's main action. "Switch account…" with ⇄ promises moving this conversation to another account.
2. **A newcomer sees** the accented "Switch account…" first in the side column, turning filled when low or out. It opens a ranked list, then a three-step wizard with fixed header and footer: Continue in Desktop or CLI, What happens as facts, Its pools (`a-wiz-6-desktop-movecopy.jpg`, `k3-session-f3.jpg`). Each choice is named and explained. The disk line is now exact (F5 PASS in `0.5.0-rewalk.md`).
3. **Value.** Exactly what D-143 asked: one visible click. But on a low account's session page the same action shows three times on one screen: the account banner's "Switch account…", the side column's button, and the rail's needs row "Switch account…" (`k3-session-f3.jpg`). PAT-SW-3 says "one visible door". The banner also offers "Remind me at 1:01 PM" (`lib/notice-words.ts:115-117`), a second reminder system that Watches ("Room again", `watches-words.ts:288`) doesn't show or manage.
4. **Unavailable.** "When it can't switch yet, it says why right under the button" (PAT-SW-5). The folded card's "4 apps can take it over; their room isn't measured yet" is honest (F10).
5. **System prompts.** Move/Copy to a Claude app profile quits and opens that app through Apple Events, which is Automation per target app. It is in AJ-E1547's list. The Move work is AJ-E2315…E2326.
6. **Surprises.** "Switch account…" also moves a conversation between apps on the same account (Continue in Desktop/CLI). That is one name by D-143, so it is not re-filed. The triple door is the surprise.

## 11. The Pools canvas (AJ-E3605, on 0.4.6's redesign)

1. **Who.** People running several agents on one project who want them to cooperate. "Pools" with a diagram promises who is in, who leads, who may message whom.
2. **A newcomer sees** a dotted canvas with member cards, "Add a member" and "Use a template", a "Lead: No lead for now" card, the header's "Setting up · 6 of 7" pill and Diagram / List / Tasks / Messages, and on the right a "Set up A · 6/7" checklist ending in "Let them message each other · Turn on team tools" (`0.5.0-rewalk-2/captures/p11-poolA.jpg`). Each step is explained; "team tools" is explained with "Learn more".
3. **Value.** Good; setup and the live view share one panel. Duplicates on the one page: "On their own" is a header dropdown ("On their own (everyone in this pool): Work together and wake each other") and also a setup row with Change. The lead is a canvas card ("No lead for now") and also a setup row ("Who leads · No lead yet").
4. **Empty.** The setup checklist is the guided setup. "Once a session joins and setup is done, this panel becomes the pool's live view" (`pages/pool/setup-words.ts:40`).
5. **System prompts.** Turning on team tools adds AI Juggler's connector for pool sessions. No macOS dialog was found in the code read. The guide says it changes nothing in the apps (`docs/guide/pools.md:261-269`).
6. **Surprises.** Two controls for one rule in view at once; twins named two ways (filed as AJ-E2314 L3).
