# Walk by hand — AI Juggler 0.2, 2026-10-28

Every screen of `release/0.2` at `422ef5b4`, walked as its target user: a
vibe coder with several subscriptions across tools who opens the app to
answer "what's running, which account has room, where is my session,
switch this before I hit the limit, what do my agents need from me".
Real data, read only, through the test bridge
([DEV-BRIDGE.md](../../DEV-BRIDGE.md)). What only a person at the window
can do is in the maintainer's checklist,
[0.2-window-checklist.md](../../0.2-window-checklist.md).

Checked against: [the screen spec](../../../review/deep/11-ux-redesign-screen-spec.md)
(§3, §4, §8–§10), [SPEC §9](../../../SPEC.md), the
[decision log](../../../review/decision-log.md) (D-25, D-35, D-36, D-45..D-49),
E21/E25's requirements, and the design rules: progressive disclosure, plain
words, every serious choice explained, never a dead-end status, readable
(body 14 px, nothing under 12 px), instant and paged, desktop-first.

## Setup

- **Build**: AI Juggler Dev from this checkout with the bridge
  (`just dev-bridge`, debug), started Finder-like (`env -i`), and Playwright's
  headless Chromium driving the page. 1440×900 and 1024×680, dark and light.
- **Data**: this Mac's real setups (six Claude Code profiles and one Codex
  home, with the Claude app and the ChatGPT app open: 8 sessions open, about
  2,200 in the last 30 days). There was no AI Juggler Dev data folder, so a
  fresh one was made the way first run's "Use them where they are" does,
  but with the scan that runs no tool (`environment_scan`) instead of first
  run's full scan, which runs each tool's `--version`. Accounts were named
  **Account A–G** so captures carry no real labels. Nothing was written to
  any tool's folder; the installed AI Juggler's data was never touched.
- **One setting changed in the Dev data**: `limits_probe_allowed = off`.
  D-39 has AI Juggler run Claude Code's `/usage` headless in every profile
  in use, by default; on a walk that rule says no tool may be started.
- **After**: the Dev app, Vite and Chromium were stopped; the Dev data folder
  was set aside as `AI Juggler Dev (walk-0.2 2026-10-28)`, as earlier walks
  did.
- **Captures**: three, redacted with `scripts/qa/redact_capture.swift` and
  checked by eye, in [`2026-10-28-0.2/`](2026-10-28-0.2/). The others stay
  on this Mac: they show real project names, an organisation's name and
  shortened addresses.

## Findings, ranked

Severity: **blocker** (stops the journey), **high** (wrong or misleading
for the persona), **medium** (slows or confuses), **low** (polish).

### F1 — A switch to an account with no room left is offered and never flagged — high

- **Screen**: session page › Continue on another account; the switch
  dialog (all three steps); New session's account list.
- **What the user sees**: "Account F 0% left" as a target like any other.
  Picking it opens "Switch to Account F", and Target, What's carried and
  Confirm say nothing about room: Confirm reads "Ready to switch to Account
  F … Nothing needs you." The targets aren't ordered by room (75, 98, 76,
  66, 0, 91 %) and none is "Suggested", although Home and Accounts already
  say "Best for new work: Account B, 98% left". New session lists Account F
  as "used most here · quiet".
- **Why it fails the persona**: the whole point of a switch is to get away
  from a limit; this lets them walk straight into another one (§4.9:
  ranked targets, headroom, "target tight + Hand over anyway").
- **Fix**: rank the targets by room (D-45 allows it on the user's own
  sheets) with "Suggested" on the best; an account out of room goes last
  with "Out of room until Thu 8:00 AM"; the Target step names the target's
  room, and an out-of-room target needs "Switch anyway".
- Capture: [w1-switch-to-account-with-no-room.jpg](2026-10-28-0.2/w1-switch-to-account-with-no-room.jpg)
- **Fixed in 040f5534**: one order by room (`ui/src/lib/room.ts`, on the
  one runway `runwayOf`) for Home's "Best for new work", Continue on
  another account, the switch card, the limit dialog and New session: most
  room first, then not measured, then low, then out of room, the best
  marked Suggested (the account Home names). Each target says its room;
  Account F reads "Out of room until Thu 08:00 · You can still pick it, but
  the conversation stops at Account F's limit straight away and waits
  there …", last and still pickable. Every switch step says the target's
  room ("Room on Account B: 98% left this week · Week resets …"); out of
  room or low is a warning with "What does this mean?" and the guide's new
  section, Confirm says "Account F is out of room: see below before you
  switch" (never "Nothing needs you"), and the button reads "Switch
  anyway". `switch_targets` comes in the ranking's order. Each step puts
  the keyboard on its heading, not on × (6c5cc6b3).

### F2 — The folded rail says "All clear" while four things need you — high

- **Screen**: the rail folded to icons (⌘B, "Show icons only").
- **What the user sees**: "All clear" and nothing else; no session icons,
  no state dots; the logo and Dev mark are cut off at the left. The bell
  says 4, Home says "3 sessions and 1 more thing need you". Still so after a
  reload.
- **Why**: the folded rail is the glance (§1.4 L1); it must never say the
  opposite of the badge (D-49: one count everywhere). The wireframes'
  convention keeps state dots and type icons in the folded rail.
- **Fix**: the folded rail reads the same backend list as the bell: the
  Needs you count and icons, then each open place's icon with its dots.
  Confirm in the window first: the bridge refuses the rail's own settings
  keys, which may play a part (checklist step 7).
- Capture: [w2-collapsed-rail-all-clear.jpg](2026-10-28-0.2/w2-collapsed-rail-all-clear.jpg)
- **Fixed in e40c6ace**: the folded rail reads the backend's one count
  (the bell's, a sign-in or the computer included; it opens Home), then
  every open session, AI Juggler's and other apps', as its app's or tool's
  mark with its state's dot and `!`, in Open now's order; "All clear" only
  when nothing needs you or is open. The logo and the Dev mark stack, both
  whole.

### F3 — Five accounts carry the same name — high

- **Fixed in 64564428**: every account is titled by the names you gave its
  profiles ("Account A"; two profiles read "A and B"), on Usage's legend
  and breakdown, Accounts, Home's Runway and Continue on another account;
  the team, sign-in and plan are the second line. Codex shows no id.

- **Screen**: Usage (the chart's legend and "By account"), Accounts and
  Home's Runway at first paint, Continue on another account's group lines.
- **What the user sees**: the subscription's title is its organisation's
  name, so five Claude accounts are all called the same thing, told apart
  only by a shortened address ("f…@", "a…@"). The Usage legend reads that
  name five times, with five colours. A sixth account on the same address
  but no organisation is "Claude account"; the Codex one is "Codex account
  · ChatGPT account …" followed by four characters of an account id.
- **Why**: "which account used what" is unanswerable on the screen built
  for it.
- **Fix**: title an account by the user's own label (the one they see
  everywhere else: rail, tiles, filters), with the organisation as a second
  line; never show an id fragment.

### F4 — Needs you counts drift apart — high (confirm in the window)

- **Screen**: the notification centre, Home's heading, the rail.
- **What the user sees** (one sitting, no reload): the centre's heading
  "Needs action · 2" over three rows; the bell "2 things need you, 2
  unread" with three unread dots; Home's heading "2 sessions need you" over
  four rows (two sessions, an account out of room, the Mac); the rail's
  Needs you frozen at its first state (its memory line still "node about 17
  GB" while Home says 9.6). One session was "Done, review it" on Home and in
  the centre but "Working" on its rail row. After a reload all four agreed.
- **Why**: D-49's one count is the maintainer's rule; a wrong badge is P0 in
  §13.1.
- **Cause, in part**: the count (`useNoticeSummary`) and the list
  (`notificationsPage`) are two reads, and the bridge doesn't forward
  `notifications://changed`, so here they drift. But Home's heading and the
  centre's heading disagree with rows on the same surface, which one event
  stream can't explain.
- **Fix**: derive each heading from the rows it sits over (one read);
  forward `notifications://changed` on the bridge (F19). Checklist step 6
  checks it live.
- Capture: [w3-centre-count-vs-rows.jpg](2026-10-28-0.2/w3-centre-count-vs-rows.jpg)
- **Fixed in e40c6ace**: the centre's "Needs action · n" and Home's
  heading count the rows they show; a row's state follows the backend's
  need, so the rail says "Done, review it" where Home and the centre do.
  The bridge forwards `notifications://changed` (cdb8cda4, F19).

### F5 — Processes counts a desktop app's processes once per session — high

- **Fixed in 64564428**: re-walked on this data: "ChatGPT · 5 sessions · 22
  processes", one "The app itself · Shared by its 5 sessions" row, each
  session "nothing of its own running"; a session is Working while its
  own processes are busy. An untitled session is named by its folder and
  start when it has one; for a desktop app's session with neither (three
  here) it reads "A session with no title yet": which window it is can't
  be known without Accessibility.

- **Screen**: Processes › Everything running.
- **What the user sees**: "ChatGPT · 5 sessions · 90 processes · 1.6 GB",
  each row "18 processes · 336 MB · 6 %", three of them "Untitled session".
  The rail says 2 sessions are open in that app; This Mac says it uses about
  1.8 GB in one window. A Claude app session marked Idle shows 223 % CPU.
  The cards say "200 running" while the tab says 194.
- **Why**: Processes is where the user decides what to stop; numbers that
  multiply the same tree send them after the wrong thing (honest fields,
  D-06).
- **Fix**: a desktop app's shared tree belongs to the app ("shared by its
  2 sessions"), counted once; per-session rows only for what that session
  started; sessions without a title say which app window they are.

### F9 — Session titles are AI Juggler's own words — medium

- **Fixed in 64564428**: AI Juggler's prompt openers live in one place
  (`juggler_core::injected`), and one rule (`prompt_title`) skips them and
  image notes for every tool; a switched session keeps its first ask. A

- **Screen**: History, a project's Sessions, the Overview's open sessions.
