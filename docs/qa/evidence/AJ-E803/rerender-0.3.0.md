## Visual baselines

The 0.3.2 re-render (AJ-E803; design pass AJ-E2814 under D-62 decision 23, plus 0.3.2's banner, picker, switch and terminal fixes): 421 changed, 0 new, 0 removed, against origin/release/0.3. Each line describes the final image against that baseline, written from looking at the pairs; identical notes share a line. Most of the change is colour: in dark, a darker canvas (#0b0e14) with cards a step lighter, sentences brighter than labels and a lighter accent (#8787f6) on links, bars and rings; on every dark screen with a top bar New session is solid violet and the logo a lighter violet, so lines don't repeat it. In light, a grey canvas (#eceef3) under white cards. Design-pass fixes show too: a one-row top bar; solid primaries in the switch dialogs; light skeletons that show on the grey page; Processes that fits at 1024; the terminal's black reaching its card's edge; and the rail's status line and row times whole ('rail times whole': a row reads 'ai-ju… · 3 h ago', cutting the project, not the time).
