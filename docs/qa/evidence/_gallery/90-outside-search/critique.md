# 90-outside-search: searching the words of sessions started outside AI Juggler, the setting and a History result

Design critique (D-57, AJ-E2814). First critique of this family, written at the 0.3.2 re-render from its captures as they are now; updated after the 0.3.2 design passes.

## 0.3.2

history is clean in both themes: the search field is focused with a violet ring, "Words are searched in every session." under it, and the one result shows its project as a green square and its profile as a magenta dot with the matching words in a sunken strip below the title; Ended and Archived (0) sit as a segmented control at the top right. settings-on shows the setting on the Privacy page with its live line ("Index: 212 MB so far · 1180 of 3412 sessions. It builds in the background."), its ticked checkbox and hint inside the raised "Usage statistics, crash reports and search" card, readable in both themes. settings-off is now re-rendered on the same 0.3.2 screen, so the pair finally compares like for like: the checkbox is empty and the line reads "Its index would take about 684 MB on this computer, for 3412 sessions." In dark the search ring, the ticked checkbox, the Desktop apps and Switching tiles and New session use the lighter accent. The second pass changed only the rail: the Refactor the settings screen row keeps its time whole in all six captures ("ai-ju… · viewed 2 min ago" where it read "viewed 2 mi…"). Both settings captures open scrolled so the usage radios above are sliced at the top edge, and the "Off" usage radio that shows is unselected, as on 84-privacy.

## Weak

- settings-on-1280x800, settings-off-1280x800 (both themes): the "Off" usage radio is unselected while usage statistics are off.
- settings-on-1280x800, settings-off-1280x800 (both themes): the capture opens with the usage option above "Off" sliced at the top edge.
