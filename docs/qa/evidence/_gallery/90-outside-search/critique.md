# 90-outside-search: searching the words of sessions started outside AI Juggler, the setting and a History result

Design critique (D-57, AJ-E2814). First critique of this family, written at the 0.3.2 re-render from its captures as they are now.

## 0.3.2

history is clean in both themes: the search field is focused with a violet ring, "Words are searched in every session." under it, and the one result shows its project as a green square and its profile as a magenta dot with the matching words in a sunken strip below the title; Ended and Archived (0) sit as a segmented control at the top right. settings-on shows the setting on the Privacy page with its live line ("Index: 212 MB so far · 1180 of 3412 sessions. It builds in the background."), its checkbox and hint inside the raised Usage statistics card, readable in both themes; the usage "Off" radio above it is unselected, as on 84-privacy. settings-off-dark and settings-off-light were not re-rendered and show a much older shell (a Home/Accounts/History/Settings/More header, the old General and Account & Cloud settings list, a pale "Connect account" button), so the off and on pair no longer shows the same screen.

## Weak

- settings-off-1280x800-dark.png, settings-off-1280x800-light.png: not re-rendered; an old app shell that doesn't match settings-on.
- settings-on-1280x800-dark.png, settings-on-1280x800-light.png: the "Off" usage radio is unselected while usage statistics are off.
