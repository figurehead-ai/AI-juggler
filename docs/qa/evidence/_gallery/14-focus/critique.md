# 14-focus: the "Go to a session" palette, and a desktop session's panel after a focus attempt and with the permission explainer

Design critique (D-57, AJ-E2814). First critique of this family, written at the 0.3.2 re-render from its captures as they are now.

## 0.3.2

palette-1024x680-light and palette-1280x800 are clear: a focused search field with a violet ring, an Actions group with "New session… ⌘N", a Sessions group with tool icon, name, account · tool and "open" at the right, and a footer of key hints; the page behind is blurred and dimmed so the palette is the only thing to read. In dark the palette's edge is soft against the blurred page but still distinct. panel-outcome-1280x800 and permission-explainer-1280x800 are pixel-for-pixel the same running Fake Desk panel with no focus outcome and no permission explainer in view, so neither proves what it is named for. That panel itself carries two solid violet buttons ("Go to it" and "Open / Focus window"). All captures were re-rendered.

## Weak

- panel-outcome-1280x800, permission-explainer-1280x800: identical captures; no outcome message or explainer is shown.
- panel-outcome-1280x800, permission-explainer-1280x800: two solid violet buttons on one panel.
