# 01-rail-density: the session rail in its three densities (comfortable, compact, minimal icons), the minimal rail's tooltip, and a row marked stale

Design critique (D-57, AJ-E2814). First critique of this family, written at the 0.3.2 re-render from its captures as they are now.

## 0.3.2

Comfortable shows two-line rows (name, coloured status · age); compact folds each row to one line with the age at the right, and minimal leaves a strip of tool icons with attention badges and a "!5" count. On the darker canvas the cards on Home stand off well and "Open its page" is the one solid violet button in the Needs you card. In compact-1024x768 and compact-1440x900 the first row's name is cut to "Fa…" and the third to "Fake D…" while the middle row shows "Fake Desk": the app tag and age squeeze the name. minimal-tooltip-1440x900 shows no tooltip at all, so it cannot prove the tooltip it is named for. stale-row-1440x900 marks its row with a small "stale · 3m" chip, which reads clearly. The "Stopped with an error · 7…" row cuts its age in comfortable-* and stale-row. All captures were re-rendered.

## Weak

- compact-1024x768, compact-1440x900: session names cut to "Fa…" and "Fake D…" by the app tag and age.
- minimal-tooltip-1440x900: no tooltip is visible.
- comfortable-*, stale-row-1440x900: "Stopped with an error · 7…" cuts its age.
- minimal-*: the minimal rail's icons are told apart only by tiny coloured corner dots and badges.
