# 55-triage: Home as a triage view: what needs you, what is parked, runway per account and what to pick up, plus the empty first-run Home

Design critique (D-57, AJ-E2814). First critique of this family, written at the 0.3.2 re-render from its captures as they are now.

## 0.3.2

On the populated Home the Needs you, Parked until a reset, Runway by account and Pick up where you left off cards stand off both canvases. "Open its page" on the top Personal row is the only solid violet button in the main area, so the next step is obvious. The amber "Asks you" and "0% left" and the violet runway bar give the right column some colour. Comfortable and compact differ only in the rail. In the compact captures the rail's first rows are cut to "Fa…" and "Fake D…", and their app badges and ages don't line up. The empty Home is clear: "Ready when you are", a Start your first session card with a solid violet New session, and the Jump to a session's own window prompt with a small solid Allow. But Runway by account sits alone in the right column with an empty left half beside it. All twelve captures were re-rendered.

## Weak

- home-populated-1440x900-compact-dark/light: in the rail, the first Fake Desk rows are cut to "Fa…" and "Fake D…", and their badges and ages sit at different x positions.
- home-empty-1440x900-dark/light and home-empty-1024x680: Runway by account sits in the right column with nothing beside it, which leaves a large empty block on the left.
- home-empty (all four): three New session buttons compete (the bar, the card and the rail).
