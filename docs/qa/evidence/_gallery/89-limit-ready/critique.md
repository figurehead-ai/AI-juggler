# 89-limit-ready: "Personal hit its limit", continue now on a ready account or wait for the reset

Design critique (D-57, AJ-E2814). First critique of this family, written at the 0.3.2 re-render from its captures as they are now.

## 0.3.2

The dialog is two option cards: the recommended "Continue now on Work Max" has a violet outline and a full-width solid violet "Continue on Work Max", a clear single primary now that the pale gradient is gone, and "Wait for the reset" has a full-width outlined "Park until 4:13 AM". It lifts well off the blurred page in both themes, the facts ("Last read in a session: 58% left · resets at 3:13 AM") sit a step dimmer than the heading, and nothing clips. The green "Ready to continue on Work Max · prepared 4 min ago" is small and, in light, a thin green on white that is the faintest line in the card though it is the reason to pick it. The copy says beta twice in one sentence ("Switching is in beta (Same conversation, new account · Beta)"), and the footer pairs an underlined "Choose another account or tool…" with a plain "Not now".

## Weak

- limit-ready-light.png: the green "Ready to continue…" line is small and low-contrast on white.
- limit-ready-dark.png, limit-ready-light.png: "Switching is in beta (… · Beta)" repeats beta.
