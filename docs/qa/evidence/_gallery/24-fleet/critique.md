# 24-fleet: the Processes page scrolled down to Automatic cleanup and Your other Macs, with a paired Mac Studio's leftover process

Design critique (D-57, AJ-E2814). First critique of this family, written at the 0.3.2 re-render from its captures as they are now; updated after the 0.3.2 design fixes.

## 0.3.2

The colour steps work here: in both dark captures the process table, the Automatic cleanup card with its three per-account panels and the Your other Macs card each stand a clear step off the near-black canvas, and in light the white cards sit on the grey page; the per-account cleanup panels read as one group of alike things, and the Work Max toggle that is on now shows the lighter dark accent. The page still arrives scrolled, though, so the table's header row is above the fold and its last row, node (astro), is sliced through the middle inside the table's own scroll area, with its status, numbers and action button cut off (all four captures). The family is named for the machine filter, yet no Machine filter control is in view in any capture; what the captures actually show is the Mac Studio row, where the disabled "Clean..." button is a faint pink outline that is hard to read next to the green "Verified owned" mark, especially in machine-filter-1440x900-light.png. The 1024x700 captures are the same images as the 1440x900 ones (1440 px wide), so the narrower width is not shown. The rail rows now read "Stopped with an error" and "Limit reached · 0% left" with nothing cut.

## Weak

- machine-filter-1024x700-dark.png, machine-filter-1440x900-light.png (all four): the machine filter the family is named for is not in view; the page is scrolled past it.
- machine-filter-1440x900-dark.png (all four): the node (astro) row is cut in half at the bottom of the process table, hiding its state and action.
- machine-filter-1440x900-light.png: the disabled "Clean..." button on the Mac Studio row is a pale pink outline with very low contrast.
- machine-filter-1024x700-*: identical to the 1440x900 captures; the narrower width is not captured.
