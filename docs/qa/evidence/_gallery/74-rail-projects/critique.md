# 74-rail-projects: the rail grouped by project, on a first-run home

Design critique (D-57, AJ-E2814). First critique of this family, written at the 0.3.2 re-render from its captures as they are now; updated after the 0.3.2 design fixes.

## 0.3.2

history-1280x800 shows the rail's Project view (Projects heading, six projects, the shopfront folder opened to two children, "Show all 6") beside a home that says "1 thing needs you". The cards stand off the page in both themes: dark cards sit a step above the near-black canvas, and in light the white Start your first session, Needs you and Runway cards read clearly on the grey canvas. The primary actions (New session in the card and in the header, Sign in, Allow) are one solid violet, so the eye goes to New session first; the rail's own New session stays a quiet outline, which is right next to it. After the design fixes the dark accent is lighter on the logo mark, the active filter button beside the search and the rail's New session outline; the top bar stays one row and nothing moved in light. The intro sentence ("Pick a project folder…") is brighter than the labels under it. The one weak spot is still the rail rows' second line, cut to "41 conversat…" and "W…", so the account at the end of each line never shows at this width.
