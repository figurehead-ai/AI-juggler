# 72-tool-view: a tool's place in the app: Settings › Accounts with Claude Code under "Tools on this computer", and the tool's header menu

Design critique (D-57, AJ-E2814). First critique of this family, written at the 0.3.2 re-render from its captures as they are now.

## 0.3.2

The overview captures now land on Settings › Accounts rather than a page of the tool's own. The Your accounts, Tools on this computer and Renewal dates cards lift off the darker or grey canvas, and New session is solid violet. Two Settings nav items look selected at once: Desktop apps carries a hover-like fill next to the real selection, Accounts. overview-1024x680 opens scrolled, so the descenders of a cut line show as a row of stray dots above "Tools on this computer". In overview-1280 the rail's "Stopped with an error · 7…" loses the age. The header-menu captures (1024 and 1280, both themes) were not re-rendered and still show the older tool page and shell, with "Add account" half hidden under the open menu.

## Weak

- overview-1024x680-dark/light: stray fragments of a cut line sit under the top bar.
- overview-*: Desktop apps and Accounts both look selected in the Settings nav.
- overview-1280x800-*: rail row "Stopped with an error · 7…" loses its age.
- header-menu-*: not re-rendered; the open menu covers the "Add account" button.
