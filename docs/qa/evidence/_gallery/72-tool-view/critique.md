# 72-tool-view: a tool's place in the app: Settings › Accounts with Claude Code under "Tools on this computer", and the tool's header menu

Design critique (D-57, AJ-E2814). First critique of this family, written at the 0.3.2 re-render from its captures as they are now; updated after the 0.3.2 design fixes.

## 0.3.2

The overview captures land on Settings › Accounts rather than a page of the tool's own. The Your accounts, Tools on this computer and Renewal dates cards lift off the darker or grey canvas, and New session is solid violet. After the design fixes the dark accent is lighter on the logo mark and the rail's New session outline, and in overview-1280 the rail's feat/read-model row now reads "Stopped with an error" whole instead of losing its age to "· 7…". Two Settings nav items still look selected at once: Desktop apps carries a hover-like fill next to the real selection, Accounts. overview-1024x680 still opens scrolled, so the descenders of a cut line show as a row of stray dots above "Tools on this computer". The header-menu captures (1024 and 1280, both themes) were not re-rendered and still show the older tool page and shell, with "Add account" half hidden under the open menu.

## Weak

- overview-1024x680-dark/light: stray fragments of a cut line sit under the top bar.
- overview-*: Desktop apps and Accounts both look selected in the Settings nav.
- header-menu-*: not re-rendered; the open menu covers the "Add account" button.
