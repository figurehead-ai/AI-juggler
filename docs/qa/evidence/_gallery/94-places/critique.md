# 94-places: Home and the rail with no, one, a typical and a busy set of sessions

Design critique (D-57, AJ-E2814). First critique of this family, written at the 0.3.2 re-render from its captures as they are now; updated after the 0.3.2 design passes.

## 0.3.2

Home at three loads (home-empty, home-typical with 8 open, home-busy with 40 open), the rail alone at 1, 8 and 40 sessions, and the rail's View menu (view), in both themes. The Your apps cards, the Needs you card and the Runway by account card stand a step off the canvas in dark and as white cards on grey in light, and the one primary per screen is solid violet (New session in home-empty, Open its page on the first Needs you row, Allow on the "Jump to a session's own window" prompt). The headline "5 sessions need you" with its "40 open · 1 inactive · 1 ended today" line gives a clear top level, and the amber "Asks you" / "Limit reached" states in the rail are readable in both themes. After the design fixes the dark captures carry the lighter accent on the logo mark, New session, Open its page, Allow and the "Measure its limits ›" links, all a touch brighter on the near-black; the light captures changed only in the top bar, which stays one row with "This Mac · Plenty of room" and the whole "7 to clean up" pill. In the Your apps row the other cards put their Open button on one line and the amber "1 needs you" chip on the line below; the ChatGPT card has no chip, so Open ChatGPT drops to that bottom line and sits lower than Open Claude and Open Terminal beside it (home-typical, home-busy). In home-busy the Work Max Claude and Terminal cards break their line as "Work Max ·" with "10 sessions" alone below, and in Needs you the fix/limits row, which has no ↗ icon, pushes its Open its page button to the right edge while the rows above and below keep theirs inset, so the buttons don't line up. In home-empty the Runway by account card still sits alone in the right-hand column with nothing beside it. The rail captures (rail-1, rail-8, rail-40) are the 264 px rail only; there the account names in group headers are cut to a few letters ("Claude · Pers…", "Claude · Work…") to make room for the warning count. After the second pass the rail rows keep their time whole and cut the project instead ("trip-not… · viewed 40 min ago" where it read "viewed 40 min a…"; "ai-ju… · viewed 2 min ago" in rail-1), and after a later fix a short project sits against its dot with no gap, but its time is cut again ("blog · 12 min …") in home-typical, rail-8 and view. The new view capture opens the rail's filter button to a View menu: Show (Inactive "in an app's list, not running", Quiet ones, Ended today, Background runs, Subagents, Other Macs, Archived) and Only (App, Profile, Account, Project, each "All ›"), with a violet focus ring on Inactive; the hints are a quiet grey beside each name and it reads well in both themes, but the Show lines carry no tick or switch, so the menu doesn't say which of them are on, and it opens over the page heading "3 sessions need you" and the rail's Account segment.

## Weak

- home-typical, home-busy: Open ChatGPT sits lower than the other cards' Open buttons, because only its card has no "1 needs you" chip and its button takes the chip's line.
- home-busy: the Claude (Work Max) and Terminal cards wrap "Work Max ·" with the dot left at the line end and "10 sessions" alone below.
- home-busy: in Needs you the fix/limits row's Open its page sits further right than the rows with a ↗ icon, so the buttons in the list don't align.
- home-empty: Runway by account sits alone in the right column, leaving a large empty area to the left under "Start your first session".
- rail-8, rail-40: group headers shorten the account to "Pers…" / "Work…" beside the ⚠ count, so two Claude groups are told apart only by their colour dot.
- home-typical, rail-8, view (both themes): the rail's "Tidy the blog's images" row reads "blog · 12 min …", its time cut though the project is short.
- view-dark, view-light: the Show lines (Inactive, Quiet ones, Ended today…) have no tick or on/off mark, so the menu doesn't show what is shown.
