# 90-processes: the Processes page, what sessions and apps run and left behind, ports, cleanup history, automatic cleanup and stopping, at 1280, 1680 and 2000

Design critique (D-57, AJ-E2814). First critique of this family, written at the 0.3.2 re-render from its captures as they are now; updated after the 0.3.2 design passes.

## 0.3.2

The four count tiles (7 to clean up in amber, 1 didn't stop in red, 1 waiting, 24 running) and the process table stand as raised cards in both themes, and the table keeps a clear hierarchy: app group rows, session rows with a dimmer summary, then processes with coloured state words and marks (amber Left behind, red Didn't stop, cyan Working) and small CPU sparklines. The design fixes show here only in dark, as the lighter accent: the logo mark, the New session buttons in the top bar and the rail, the "Left running" pills on Ports, the on switch for Work Max's "Tidy left-behind dev servers" (ended-*, history-*, rules-preview) and the selected radio in the stop dialog are a brighter violet; the light captures are unchanged apart from sub-pixel shifts in the top bar, where session-tab-1680x1000 keeps "This Mac · Plenty of room" and the whole "7 to clean up" pill on one row. At 1280 the Owner column drops; after the second pass the columns are re-spaced, so group summaries are whole ("payments · 4 processes · 3.0 GB · 3 %") and Ports sits a clear gap from Memory, and with State now 11rem wide "Left behind while closed" ends with a clear gap before the CPU figure in running-* and attention-* at 1280 in both themes. At 1680 and 2000 the only change is a wider gap before Ports; at 2000 the page is a centred column with wide gutters and its 12 px type looks small. In every light capture with Automatic cleanup (ended-*, history-*, ports-*, rules-preview) the off switches are still pale grey tracks on white that nearly vanish. other-apps opens with the table scrolled so the first visible row's top half sits under the sticky column header, the filters popover ends on its "App" heading with the options below it cut, and large's group row reads "2500 %" CPU, a sum that looks like an error. The stop dialogs use a coral (dark) or red (light) destructive button that names the PID and port ("Stop vite (PID 8123) and free :5173"), readable, but their radios still sit at the dialog's right edge, far from "This process only" and the other labels. The rail's status lines show whole ("Stopped with an error", "Probably stuck"), and after the second pass the Open now row keeps its age too: "ai-j… · 3 h ago Probably asks you" where it read "ai-jugg… · 3 …".
