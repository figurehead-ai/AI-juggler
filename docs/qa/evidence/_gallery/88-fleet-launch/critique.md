# 88-fleet-launch: Fleet, starting a session on another Mac, and Usage split by machine

Design critique (D-57, AJ-E2814). First critique of this family, written at the 0.3.2 re-render from its captures as they are now.

## 0.3.2

remote-sheet is a clear chooser: Runs on as three chips with Mac mini outlined in violet, folder tiles and account rows with the chosen one tinted, a footer that says what happens ("Mac mini checks it and starts it there.") and "Start there →" as the single solid violet button; the dialog lifts well off the blurred page in both themes. usage-by-machine moves the This Mac status into the rail ("This Mac" heading, "Mac mini · 1 open" at the bottom) and adds a Fleet tab to the header; the session list keeps names, account dots, right-aligned tokens and costs on one grid, and the Monthly caps and "Last 24 hours, by machine" cards sit side by side as raised cards, with MacBook Air honestly "Hasn't reported yet". The list writes costs as "~$98" while the machine card writes "≈ $6.42", two signs for the same kind of estimate. In light the olive Work Max dot is dull on white but still distinct from Personal's magenta; nothing clips or overlaps.

## Weak

- usage-by-machine-1280x800-dark.png, usage-by-machine-1280x800-light.png: estimates use "~$" in the session list and "≈ $" in "Last 24 hours, by machine".
- remote-sheet-1280x800-dark.png, remote-sheet-1280x800-light.png: "Its launch setting's folder" sits alone in the second row of folder tiles, leaving half the row empty.
