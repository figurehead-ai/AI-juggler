# 77-menus: every row menu, the palette's action list and a submenu

Design critique (D-57, AJ-E2814). First critique of this family, written at the 0.3.2 re-render from its captures as they are now; updated after the 0.3.2 design passes.

## 0.3.2

The menus share one pattern: a small header naming the object, icon rows grouped by rules, and the destructive item (Remove…, Delete…, Forget…, Stop this session) last in red. In dark the menus are a lighter surface than the page and in light they are white with a shadow over the grey canvas, so they stand out in both. The session menus are now wide enough for their longest label: "Continue on another account…" is whole beside its ⌘⇧H shortcut in session-menu, session-menu-greyed and session-submenu. session-menu-greyed explains its disabled items in place ("It has no conversation to carry over yet"); after the second pass the rail is scrolled to its Needs you rows and the menu opens beside the clicked feat/read-model row instead of from the top of the window (at about 245 px in light, 200 px in dark), so the Needs you rows above it stay visible (the rail's search and App/Project/Account switch are scrolled out of view); but its top edge cuts through the lower half of that row's own name, its bottom edge half-covers the name of a later feat/read-model row, and in light the rail's first visible row shows only "Asks you", its name scrolled under the header. session-submenu opens "Snooze or mute" to the side with a focus ring on "For 15 minutes". palette-actions opens with Go to it highlighted under the search field ("Act on feat/read-model, or search…") and a cut row at the bottom hinting at more; after the second pass the square-cornered violet ring around the search field is gone in both themes, but nothing replaces it, so no ring, caret or underline shows that typing goes into the field. In dark the lighter accent shows on the logo mark, New session and the palette's Go to it. In account-menu and history-menu the rail's feat/read-model row now keeps its time whole ("ai-j… · 3 h ago" where it read "ai-jugg… · 3 …"). The project menu in project-menu still covers the rail rows it opens over, and account-menu's menu covers its own card's row.

## Weak

- project-menu-1280x800 (both themes): the menu sits over the rail rows it belongs to, cutting "Fake Desk app" to "app" and "Stopped with an error" to "Stopped with an e".
- palette-actions-1280x800 (both themes): the search field shows no focus at all (no ring, caret or underline), so only the highlighted Go to it hints where the keyboard is.
- session-menu-greyed-1280x800 (both themes): the menu's top edge cuts through the lower half of the clicked feat/read-model row's name in the rail, and its bottom edge half-covers a later feat/read-model row's name.
