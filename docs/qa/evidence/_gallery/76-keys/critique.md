# 76-keys: the keyboard shortcuts sheet and the folded rail on a session page

Design critique (D-57, AJ-E2814). First critique of this family, written at the 0.3.2 re-render from its captures as they are now.

## 0.3.2

sheet-1280x800 lays the shortcuts out in two columns (Anywhere, Screens) with key caps right-aligned on thin rules; it is easy to scan, and the dark sheet now sits a step above the dimmed page. At 1024x680 the sheet is shorter than its list: the last visible row's Esc cap is clipped and "Open a row's menu" and "Open a session's page" are hidden below with no scroll cue. folded-1024x680 shows the session page with the rail folded to marks, and folded-1280x800 the full page; the "Where it runs" and Runway cards lift off the canvas in both themes, and the terminal sits as a dark block inside a white card in light, which reads fine. The rail in folded-1280x800 shows "Stopped with an error · 7…", with the age cut off.

## Weak

- sheet-1024x680 (both themes): the list is cut at the sheet's bottom edge with no hint that two more shortcuts are below.
- folded-1280x800 (both themes): the rail row "Stopped with an error · 7…" loses its age.
