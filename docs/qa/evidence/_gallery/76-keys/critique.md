# 76-keys: the keyboard shortcuts sheet and the folded rail on a session page

Design critique (D-57, AJ-E2814). First critique of this family, written at the 0.3.2 re-render from its captures as they are now; updated after the 0.3.2 design fixes.

## 0.3.2

sheet-1280x800 lays the shortcuts out in two columns (Anywhere, Screens) with key caps right-aligned on thin rules; it is easy to scan, and the dark sheet sits a step above the dimmed page. After the design fixes the close button's focus ring is in the lighter dark accent (sheet, both sizes). At 1024x680 the sheet is still shorter than its list: the last visible row's Esc cap is clipped at the sheet's bottom edge, and "Open a row's menu" and "Open a session's page" are hidden below with no scroll cue. folded-1024x680 shows the session page with the rail folded to marks, and folded-1280x800 the full page; the "Where it runs" and Runway cards lift off the canvas in both themes, and the terminal sits as a dark block inside a white card in light, which reads fine. The rail in folded-1280x800 now reads "Stopped with an error" whole, without the cut-off age, and the logo mark and the rail's New session outline take the lighter accent.

## Weak

- sheet-1024x680 (both themes): the list is cut at the sheet's bottom edge (the Esc cap is half clipped) with no hint that two more shortcuts are below.
