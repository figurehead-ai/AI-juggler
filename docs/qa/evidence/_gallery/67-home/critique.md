# 67-home: Home in its states (welcome, no sessions, all clear, sessions that need you) with the legend, the account menu and the rail's View menu

Design critique (D-57, AJ-E2814). First critique of this family, written at the 0.3.2 re-render from its captures as they are now.

## 0.3.2

The Needs you, Runway by account and Pick up where you left off cards now stand off the canvas, and "Open its page" is the one solid violet button in the Needs you card. The welcome and no-accounts screens have a strong headline and a solid "Find my accounts", but with the header's New session and the solid Allow there are three violet fills competing on one screen. The legend popover and the account menu (more-menu) read well in both themes. rail-filters-open opens with the heading "5 sessions need you" cut in half under the top bar, and its View menu gives no on/off mark for the Show items. At 390 px the header takes three rows and the Needs you rows cut their status to a few letters; welcome-how opens with its first card's title cut under the bar. All captures were re-rendered.

## Weak

- rail-filters-open-1280x800-dark/light: the page heading is cut under the top bar; the View menu shows no checked state for its Show items.
- no-accounts-*, welcome-*: three solid violet buttons (header New session, Find my accounts, Allow) on one screen.
- needs-you-390x844-*, all-clear-390x844-*: three-row header; row status cut to a few letters; the permission card's title wraps word by word.
- welcome-how-1280x800-*: the first card's title is cut under the top bar.
