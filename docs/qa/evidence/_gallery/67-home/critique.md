# 67-home: Home in its states (welcome, no sessions, all clear, sessions that need you) with the legend, the account menu and the rail's View menu

Design critique (D-57, AJ-E2814). First critique of this family, written at the 0.3.2 re-render from its captures as they are now; updated after the 0.3.2 design fixes.

## 0.3.2

The Needs you, Runway by account and Pick up where you left off cards stand off the canvas, and "Open its page" is the one solid violet button in the Needs you card. The welcome and no-accounts screens have a strong headline and a solid "Find my accounts", but with the header's New session and the solid Allow there are still three violet fills competing on one screen. After the design fixes the dark accent is lighter on the logo mark, the welcome icon, the rail's New session outline and the Work Max runway bar; the 1280 and 1440 top bars stay one row in both themes. The biggest change is at 390 px: the cleanup pill is now a broom with "7", so the header drops from three rows to two (tabs, then Usage and History beside the broom, New session, bell and avatar), and the page starts about 35 px higher. The Needs you rows at 390 still cut their status to "Limit reach…" and "Stopped wi…", because each row's button takes half the card. The legend popover and the account menu (more-menu) read well in both themes. In rail-filters-open the View menu still covers the lower half of the "5 sessions need you" heading and gives no on/off mark for its Show items. welcome-how still opens with the first card's title cut under the top bar. In the rail's Open now group the third row's meta reads "ai-jugg… · 3 …", cut in two places.

## Weak

- rail-filters-open-1280x800-dark/light: the View menu covers the lower half of the "5 sessions need you" heading; it shows no checked state for its Show items.
- no-accounts-*, welcome-*: three solid violet buttons (header New session, Find my accounts, Allow) on one screen.
- needs-you-390x844-*: the Needs you rows cut their status to a few letters ("Limit reach…", "Stopped wi…").
- all-clear-390x844-*: the permission card's title wraps word by word ("Jump to a / session's own / window").
- welcome-how-1280x800-*: the first card's title ("Jump to a session's own window") is cut under the top bar.
- more-menu-1280x800-*, rail-filters-open-1280x800-*: the rail's feat/read-model row under Open now reads "ai-jugg… · 3 …", cut twice.
