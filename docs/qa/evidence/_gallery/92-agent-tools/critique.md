# 92-agent-tools: agent tools, the question on a pool's canvas and the setting with where they are off

Design critique (D-57, AJ-E2814). First critique of this family, written at the 0.3.2 re-render from its captures as they are now.

## 0.3.2

agent-tools-question puts the question card ("Let agents in your projects see each other and work together?") above the Checkout redesign canvas with one solid violet "Yes, let them" and an outlined "Not now", a clear single primary; the dotted canvas holds the lead node (feat/read-model, Lead, Working) and the Activity panel on the right lists This pool's facts and an honest empty Live state, readable in both themes. "ADD A SESSION 0 ▾" in spaced capitals looks like a section label rather than a control. settings-agent-tools shows "Agent tools in the sessions AI Juggler starts" ticked and a "Where they're off · 1 off" fold whose Projects and Profiles checklists tick the ones that do get the tools, so under a heading about where they're off a tick means on; the unticked ai-juggler is the one that is off. The top of the capture shows a stray row of dots from the content above the edge; cards and checklists otherwise sit cleanly on the canvas in both themes.
