# 95-app-icons: installed apps' own icons on Home, in the rail, in History and in the Go to session palette

Design critique (D-57, AJ-E2814). First critique of this family, written at the 0.3.2 re-render from its captures as they are now; updated after the 0.3.2 design fixes.

## 0.3.2

The app marks do their job at every size: Claude's and Terminal's own icons lead the Your apps cards (home-1280, home-2000), the rail's group headers (rail-1280, rail-2000), the History rows (history-1280, history-2000) and the Go to session palette's rows (palette-1280, palette-2000), with the profile's colour dot beside the name rather than on the icon. ChatGPT has no icon in this fixture and falls back to a neutral lettered "C" tile, which sits quietly beside the real icons and does not look broken. The app cards and the Needs you and Runway cards stand off the dark canvas and read as white cards on grey in light; Open its page is the solid violet primary, and the amber "1 needs you" chips and "Asks you" labels stay legible in both themes. In dark the lighter accent shows on the logo mark, the header's New session, the rail's New session label and History's focused search ring; the top bar stays one row with "This Mac · Plenty of room" and "7 to clean up" whole at 1280 and 2000. The palettes are re-rendered now on the 0.3.2 palette: "Go to a session or screen, or type what to do…", an Actions group (New session… ⌘N, lit) above Sessions, an icon and "Work Max · in Claude" on every row, "open" on the right and "↩ open its page · ⌘↩ go to its app" in the footer; the last row ("Fix the flaky login test") is cut by the list's bottom edge, which reads as "scroll for more". As elsewhere on Home, the ChatGPT card, the only one without a "1 needs you" chip, drops Open ChatGPT to the chip's line, so it sits lower than the other cards' Open buttons (home-1280, home-2000). The rail still cuts its meta lines ("viewed 40 min a…", "4 min a…", "ai-juggl… · 2 …") in rail-1280, rail-2000 and every Home and History capture.

## Weak

- home-1280, home-2000: Open ChatGPT sits lower than Open Claude and Open Terminal in the same row of cards.
- rail-1280, rail-2000 (and the rail on home and history): the meta lines cut the age ("trip-notes · viewed 40 min a…", "payments-api · 4 min a…", "ai-juggl… · 2 …").
- history-2000-dark: the two rows sit at the top of a wide centred list with the rest of the page empty; fine for two rows, thin for a real history.
