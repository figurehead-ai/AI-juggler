# 95-app-icons: installed apps' own icons on Home, in the rail, in History and in the Go to session palette

Design critique (D-57, AJ-E2814). First critique of this family, written at the 0.3.2 re-render from its captures as they are now; updated after the 0.3.2 design passes.

## 0.3.2

The app marks do their job at every size: Claude's and Terminal's own icons lead the Your apps cards (home-1280, home-2000), the rail's group headers (rail-1280, rail-2000), the History rows (history-1280, history-2000) and the Go to session palette's rows (palette-1280, palette-2000), with the profile's colour dot beside the name rather than on the icon. ChatGPT has no icon in this fixture and falls back to a neutral lettered "C" tile, which sits quietly beside the real icons and does not look broken. The app cards and the Needs you and Runway cards stand off the dark canvas and read as white cards on grey in light; Open its page is the solid violet primary, and the amber "1 needs you" chips and "Asks you" labels stay legible in both themes. In dark the lighter accent shows on the logo mark, the header's New session, the rail's New session label and History's focused search ring; the top bar stays one row with "This Mac · Plenty of room" and "7 to clean up" whole at 1280 and 2000. The palettes are re-rendered now on the 0.3.2 palette: "Go to a session or screen, or type what to do…", an Actions group (New session… ⌘N, lit) above Sessions, an icon and "Work Max · in Claude" on every row, "open" on the right and "↩ open its page · ⌘↩ go to its app" in the footer; the last row ("Fix the flaky login test") is cut by the list's bottom edge, which reads as "scroll for more". As elsewhere on Home, the ChatGPT card, the only one without a "1 needs you" chip, drops Open ChatGPT to the chip's line, so it sits lower than the other cards' Open buttons (home-1280, home-2000). After the second pass the rail keeps every time whole and cuts the project instead ("trip-not… · viewed 40 min ago", "ai-ju… · viewed 2 min ago", "payments-… · 4 min ago", "ai-… · 2 min ago") in rail-1280, rail-2000 and every Home and History capture; the palettes changed only in the blurred rail behind them. Two small costs show: where the project is short the separator floats away from it ("blog   · 12 min ago"), and "2 min ago" runs into "Probably asks you" with no gap in history-2000-dark (with only a hairline gap in rail-2000). rail-1280-dark now opens with a Needs you group above Open now (Migrate the billing webh…, Answer the release ques…, Tidy the blog's images, each with its state and an open-in-app mark), whose meta lines are cut ("payments-api · Claude · Wo…"); Open now and its groups move down under it.

## Weak

- home-1280, home-2000: Open ChatGPT sits lower than Open Claude and Open Terminal in the same row of cards.
- history-2000-dark (nearly so in rail-2000-*): the Answer the release question row's "ai-… · 2 min ago" touches "Probably asks you" with no gap.
- rail-*, home-*, history-*: on a short project the "·" separator sits apart from it ("blog   · 12 min ago"), so the meta lines don't read evenly.
- rail-1280-dark: the Needs you rows cut their meta ("payments-api · Claude · Wo…", "ai-juggler · Terminal · Work …") and titles ("Migrate the billing webh…").
- history-2000-dark: the two rows sit at the top of a wide centred list with the rest of the page empty; fine for two rows, thin for a real history.
