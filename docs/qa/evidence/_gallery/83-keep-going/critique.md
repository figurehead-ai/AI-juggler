# 83-keep-going: restarting crashed sessions and keeping the Mac awake

Design critique (D-57, AJ-E2814). First critique of this family, written at the 0.3.2 re-render from its captures as they are now.

## 0.3.2

settings-keep-going groups the options under "Keep work going" as checkboxes with a sentence each (restart sessions that crash, keep the Mac awake, on battery too, reopen AI Juggler, open at login); the settings card stands off the canvas in both themes and the explanations are brighter than before. launch-setting-restart shows the "If a session crashes: Restart it" choice inside a launch setting, with solid violet Launch buttons and a pale, clearly disabled Save overrides. this-mac-awake is meant to show that the Mac is kept awake, but its "Kept awake while 1 AI Juggler…" line sits at the very bottom of the AI Juggler card and is cut by the window edge, so the state it documents is half visible.

## Weak

- this-mac-awake-1280x800 (both themes): the "Kept awake while 1 AI Juggler…" line is cut by the bottom of the window.
