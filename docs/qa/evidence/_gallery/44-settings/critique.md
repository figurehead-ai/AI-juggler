# 44-settings: Settings › Advanced, the Figurehead & Cloud card from signed out to pairing code, linking, connected and unlink

Design critique (D-57, AJ-E2814). First critique of this family, written at the 0.3.2 re-render from its captures as they are now.

## 0.3.2

The cards now stand off the page in both themes (white on the grey canvas in light, a step lighter in dark), and in pairing-code the solid violet Connect account is the one clear primary next to the outlined Use a pairing code and the pale disabled Link this Mac. linking shows the QVM3-GABM code large in a mono box with Open the page again and Cancel, and connected lays out the three Cloud features with bold "Sends:" lines that make each one's cost easy to scan. unlink-confirm is a small, readable popover with a red-tinted Unlink beside Cancel. But consent, paid-locked-offline, signed-out and unlinked all show the top of Advanced (the Cleanup card with its per-account checkboxes) and none of the Figurehead & Cloud card, so these four captures don't show the state they are named for. In connected (light) the off switch for Sync usage stats is a white track on a white card and almost disappears. Every capture was re-rendered.

## Weak

- consent, paid-locked-offline, signed-out, unlinked (all sizes, both themes): the capture stops at the Cleanup section and never reaches the Figurehead & Cloud card, so the named state isn't visible.
- connected-light, unlink-confirm-light: the off switch for "Sync usage stats to my dashboard" is nearly invisible on the white card.
- connected, unlink-confirm: the page is scrolled so the section heading is gone and the card starts cut under the top bar.
- Rail at 1280 (all captures): "Stopped with an error · 7 …" cuts the age.
