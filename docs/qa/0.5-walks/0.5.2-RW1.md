## Rules (read first; a broken rule stops the walk and is reported, never worked around)

1. **Only AI Juggler Dev and the lab.** Never the installed AI Juggler or AI Juggler Nightly (both run now; the same process name, `juggler-app`: address Dev by its pid, never by name), never the Mac's real Claude, Codex or ChatGPT apps or a real profile.
2. **Read-only on everything real.** Nothing is written under the real `ClaudeProfiles`, `Claude`, `.claude`, `.codex` or `ClaudeCodeProfiles` folders. Credential files and Keychain items are never opened. Reading the lab's own files (`<lab>`, read-only, `sqlite3 "file:…?mode=ro"`) is allowed.
3. **No sign-ins, no system Allow presses by the walker.** A system dialog is photographed and logged (K1), never answered; where a step needs one answered, it says MAINTAINER and the walker stops and reports. AI Juggler's own priming sheets: press **Not now**, except D2's "Start Bot's AI helper" sheet, whose **Continue** is AI Juggler's own button (if macOS then shows a dialog, don't touch it: stop and report). Home's "Jump to a session's own window" card: **Not now** only.
4. **Every keystroke guarded.** All input goes through `<D>` (run `<D> guard` first), which checks that Dev is frontmost and topmost at the point before every key, click and wheel click. Never type with another tool. A `PAUSED`/`STOPPED` line means the maintainer is at the Mac: wait. Quitting Dev is `<Q> <pid>` only (Dev's own Quit; `<D>` refuses ⌘Q); a quit sheet that follows is pressed with `<D>`.
5. **Tool-folder snapshot before and after** (paths, sizes, mtimes only, never contents): `AJ_LAB_OUT=/tmp/aij-rw1-tools/snap scripts/qa/runtime_lab_snapshot.sh take before <lab> <the real tool folders, listed>` at A1, `take after` at Z2, then `diff before after`. A change under a real folder that the walk caused is a blocker (the maintainer's own running apps write there all the time: say which, as PL1's Z2 did).
6. **D-37: never read another process's environment** (no `ps eww`, `ps -E`, `launchctl getenv`). Processes are listed by pid and program name only (`ps -o pid=,comm=`; `runtime_lab_snapshot.sh procs`).
7. **Decline every download.** No Download button is pressed (Qwen, Granite, Nemotron, Gemma: looked at, never pressed); no curl, npx, pip or brew.
8. **Reap every launched process.** Every Dev launch is in the Launched table when started and shown gone at Z3 (`lsof -t +D <lab>` prints nothing once Dev is quit, `pgrep -f juggler-apple-ai` lists no Dev helper). No blank window is left.
9. **Fill every Result cell before any teardown.** The lab is torn down only at Z4, after Opus has checked the verdicts.
10. Every launch goes through `scripts/qa/lab_env.sh` (the launch line above does).
11. Record against the step's number. Result is `PASS`, `FAIL (finding)` or `BLOCKED (why)`. A step that fails is still followed by the rest; the whole walk is repeated once the findings are fixed (`docs/qa/persona-validation.md`: one findings list, triaged once). **Feedback over 100 ms, and anything over 1 s with no progress cue, is noted in the Record cell with its time** (from the driver's log: a press's line to the first `texts`/`wait` that shows the change; `<D> wait TEXT` prints "shown after N ms").
12. Captures: opened by eye before they are kept; real names redacted only by `scripts/qa/redact_capture.swift` through `<D> shot`; named `S<step>-<what>-<light|dark>.jpg`. Settings › Advanced shows this Mac's real name ("This Mac's name on Figurehead"): a capture there must cover it (`--box`), and `shot` refuses one that still shows the account's name.
13. **The walker never sleeps the Mac, never changes a system setting** (appearance, notifications, Apple Intelligence), and never changes the Mac's clock. G7 is a handshake: stop, report, wait.
14. **The digest takes finished posts in.** About a minute after the first of several finishes, the feed's digest item ("AI Juggler · Digest · N sessions changed") takes the finished posts in (V3). Read a finished post's diff stat and review **at once after `/exit`**, then again after the digest, and record both.
