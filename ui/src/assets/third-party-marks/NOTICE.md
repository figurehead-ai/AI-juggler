# Third-party marks: not covered by AI Juggler's MIT licence

The names and logos of other companies and their products belong to
their owners. AI Juggler uses them only to say which tools it works with;
that implies no endorsement by, or affiliation with, their owners.
See TRADEMARKS.md at the repository's root.

A mark is added here only when its owner's guidelines allow its use in
a third-party app, or the owner has given written permission. Each is
recorded in `docs/research/brand-marks.md` with its source, its licence,
its guidelines and the date it was checked. It is drawn as its owner
allows, in `currentColor` by default; no mark is altered beyond that.
Everything here is bundled with the app, and nothing is fetched at
runtime.

Today no third-party mark is bundled: Anthropic's and OpenAI's
guidelines both ask for approval first (checked 2026-10-21), so their
tools show AI Juggler's own neutral letter mark.
