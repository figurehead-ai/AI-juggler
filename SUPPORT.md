# Getting help

- **Questions and ideas:** [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/figurehead-ai/AI-juggler/discussions).
- **Bugs:** open an [issue](https://github.com/figurehead-ai/AI-juggler/issues/new/choose)
  with the bug template. Include your OS, the AI Juggler version or
  commit, and steps to reproduce.
- **Security problems:** never in public — see [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md).

Before posting logs or screenshots, remove anything personal: account
names, e-mail addresses, home-folder paths, tokens. AI Juggler never
needs your vendor credentials to diagnose a problem — please don't
share them.
