# Getting help

Start with the [README's FAQ and troubleshooting](./README.md#faq) and the
[user guide](./docs/guide/). Most questions are answered there.

- **Questions and setup help:** [Discussions › Q&A](https://github.com/figurehead-ai/AI-juggler/discussions/categories/q-a).
- **Ideas:** [Discussions › Ideas](https://github.com/figurehead-ai/AI-juggler/discussions/categories/ideas).
- **Bugs:** open an [issue](https://github.com/figurehead-ai/AI-juggler/issues/new/choose)
  with the bug template. Include:
  - the AI Juggler version and channel (Settings › About & updates);
  - your macOS version, and whether your Mac is Apple silicon or Intel;
  - the versions of the tools involved (`claude --version`, `codex --version`);
  - the steps that cause the problem;
  - if you can, the diagnostics: Settings › General › Report a problem ›
    **Export diagnostics…** shows every file before you save it, with names,
    paths and keys taken out.
- **Security problems:** never in public. See [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md).

AI Juggler is maintained by a small team, so answers can take a few days.
Bugs that affect your data or your accounts come first.

Before posting logs or screenshots, remove anything personal: account names,
email addresses, home-folder paths and tokens. AI Juggler never needs your
credentials to diagnose a problem, so please don't share them.
