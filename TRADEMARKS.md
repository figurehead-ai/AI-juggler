names and logos are not. They tell people that a build or a page comes
from this project, so they stay with the project. The MIT License
doesn't cover them, and contributing doesn't grant rights to them.

- The AI Juggler logo and app icon: the image files in

The names and logos of the AI tools AI Juggler works with, and of the
companies behind them, belong to their owners. AI Juggler uses them only
to say which tools it works with; that implies no endorsement by, or
affiliation with, their owners. They aren't covered by the MIT License

[`ui/src/assets/third-party-marks/`](./ui/src/assets/third-party-marks/)

source and guidelines are recorded in
[`docs/research/brand-marks.md`](./docs/research/brand-marks.md). The app
icon's own notice is [`crates/juggler-app/icons/NOTICE.md`](./crates/juggler-app/icons/NOTICE.md).

## What you can do without asking

- Use, study, change and share the code under the MIT License, for any
  purpose.
- Refer to the project truthfully by name: "works with AI Juggler",
  "a plugin for AI Juggler", "based on AI Juggler", "a fork of AI
  Juggler".
- Share unmodified source or builds from this repository, with the name
  and icon as they are.
- Write about it, review it, teach it, and link to it, using the name
  and logo to identify it.

## What needs permission first

- Shipping a modified version under the AI Juggler or Figurehead name or
  icon. You may change the code; a changed build needs its own name and
  icon.
- Using the names or logos in your own product, company, domain or
  account names, or anything that suggests you are the project, are
  endorsed by it, or are an official build.
- Changing the logo, or combining it with your own marks.
- Merchandise.

## If you fork

A fork that ships its own builds should:

- use its own name and icon. Replace the files in
  `crates/juggler-app/icons/`, the `productName` and `identifier` in
  `crates/juggler-app/tauri.conf.json`, and the logo mark in the UI;
- keep the MIT License notice, as the license requires;
- say "based on AI Juggler" if you like. That's fair use of the name.

## Asking

Ask in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/figurehead-ai/AI-juggler/discussions)
or open an issue titled "Trademark question". If you're unsure whether a
use is fine, ask; we'd rather say yes than surprise anyone.
